THE TRANSFORMATION (Ismara branch, standalone)
He steps toward her, into what light the fire throws this far.
The skin along his jaw splits first, parting cleanly, straight back toward each ear. What's underneath is grey, and it moves on its own. The mandible beneath grinds forward out of its socket, extending three inches, then locks with a crack and finds a new hinge point.
His eyes pull backward into the skull as the socket itself reshapes. The orbital bone shifts visibly under skin gone the color of tallow, until what looks out at her sits a full inch deeper than it should, the socket wet at the rim.
His spine goes next -- vertebra grinds audibly against vertebra as it pushes his frame taller. Skin splits along both sides of his ribcage before the muscle beneath has finished growing to fill it. Bare organ shows for one full second before it closes over, wet and glistening.
His fingers are last. Each one breaks at the middle knuckle, folding back against its own joint. The bone underneath keeps growing past the break, punching a new joint into place and driving a black claw out through what's left of the fingertip. One at a time. Unhurried.
Two long ridges travel up beneath the skin of his back before they break through at the spine. The skin over them gives, and the membrane unfolds into the firelight, deep purple-black, still stretching taut in patches.
He holds there a moment, wings spread, before he rises -- level with her eyes first, unhurried, then higher.
He doesn't go straight south. He circles once, low, over the fire, over every upturned face.
"My thanks for the hospitality," he calls down -- the same courteous register he's used all night, carrying easily over the wind of his own wings. Then he climbs, and is gone south.

GM RULES -- THE TEMPORAL WEATHER (proximity zones + the alarm)
The distortion is a SIDE EFFECT: the cult corrupts the TOWER from the undercroft; the mountain's existing Time-Wound turns that violence into weather. The cult does not target the Wound and does not control the weather -- they just know how to surf it. Each scene below restates its own zone at the point of use; this is the master card.
HOW IT RUNS (core, locked):
1. The GM sets a REAL-WORLD ALARM (phone, hidden). It fires with NO warning;
the players learn to dread the sound.
2. On the alarm: GM rolls d20 -> picks which table in the CURRENT ZONE's set is
active ('the weather shifts'). Announce nothing but the sensation.
3. In combat, every character rolls d20 TEMPO at the start of their turn on the
active table -> that is their action economy this turn.
4. THE GRADIENT = two dials: higher zones have NASTIER tables AND the alarm
fires MORE OFTEN.
5. SEPARATION IS EXPOSURE: anyone who strays from the group slips out of the
shared 'now' -- they face this zone's weather ALONE, no one to pull them
back into sequence. A tight, roped body is the only anchor.
ENEMIES CHEAT: Dagonites carry grafted TIDE-SHARDS (obsidian slivers fused
at the sternum) -- they choose their Tempo result instead of rolling. THE
COUNTER: destroy the shard -- a Called Shot (Very Hard -6), or Sunder aimed at

THE TEN EMBLEMS -- the spine of the climb (READ ALOUD, never gated behind a roll)
Ten etched tablets stand along the 7,000 Steps. Together they are the mountain's record of the LAST time the world nearly ended and someone stopped it -- the Dragon War, and how Men won it with the Voice. They are load-bearing: read each one ALOUD when the party reaches it. No Notice check, no gate. The story IS the climb's spine.
THE DROWNING GRADIENT: the working is in the undercroft BENEATH the monastery -- so the sap is thinnest at the bottom of the climb and worst at the top. It crawls UP each stone against gravity and stands in the cuts, which means on a partly-covered tablet the LOWEST lines go first and the top line survives longest. The party gets the beginning of the story clean and loses the end. By the upper Steps the tablets say nothing at all -- so the party never reads the Greybeards' own doctrine off the stone. They can only get it from Arngeir's mouth.

THE HIDDEN LAYER -- the climb's puzzle (this is SECTION 2's puzzle; it resolves at the end of 3C)
THE MECHANISM: broken time does not put readers in different ERAS. Every reader stands in an EQUALLY corrupted moment -- but a different PERSONAL now, so the SAME degree of ruin lands on different words. Two people reading the same stone at the same time get different survivors. This sits ALONGSIDE the canon inscriptions above -- it is not a replacement, and it is NOT blocked by the physical sap (a personal-now reading is not a reading of the physical stone, so it works even on the fully drowned Emblems VIII-X).
