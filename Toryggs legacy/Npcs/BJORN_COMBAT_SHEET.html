Passive Traits

The Steed Stone Stride Passive

Bjorn sprints through deep drifts, ice rime, and frozen shale at full speed without rolling Agility or paying terrain movement penalties.

Dual Flurry (Expert Perk) Passive

The FP cost of his Fighting Stance and dual-weapon attacks is permanently reduced to 0 FP. He can sustain relentless multi-attack tempo every turn without stamina loss.

The Unaged Throat (Latent Surge) Passive • At 0 HP

If reduced to 0 HP, Bjorn does not collapse immediately. An unvoiced kinetic shockwave (Fus resonance) erupts from his chest, staggering adjacent enemies 10ft and granting 1 final dying turn to shield an ally.
