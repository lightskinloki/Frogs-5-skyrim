Passive Anatomical Gifts

The 206 Acoustic Bones Passive

The skeleton acts as an acoustic horn tuned to the frequencies of unmaking. Alfonso automatically perceives tonal dissonance, leylines, planar shear, and Daedric ritual anchors within 60 feet as resonant vibrations rattling in his jaw and ribs.

Tempo Synchronization (Dagonite Tide-Shards) Passive

Forged from the obsidian shards of the Dagonite cult, Alfonso's bones vibrate in harmony with temporal distortion. Inside Zone 3 temporal weather or planar shear zones, he rolls Tempo checks with Advantage (or may choose In Step on a standard roll).

Pestilent Joint-Seepage (Peryite's Blessing) Passive

Virulent green-black bile and spore-veins weep continuously between the obsidian joints. Any melee attacker striking Alfonso with unarmed strikes or natural weapons must make a Hard Might Save (-4) or contract the Forelhost Living Strain.

Alfonso aligns his skeletal arm with the rapier and discharges a concentrated beam of black-green pestilence and razor-wind.

Roll Magic (TN 19). Deals 22 Magic/Disease Damage to all creatures in the line. Targets who fail an Agility roll are knocked prone.

Razor Claw / Bone-Cleave
Major Action • Melee 0 FP

Sweeps with razor-honed obsidian talons that chip volcanic glass into the wound.

Roll Agility (TN 14) or Might (TN 14). Deals 12 Slashing/Piercing Damage. Ignores 3 points of enemy DR (Razor Sharp) and inflicts 2 Bleed/round for 2 rounds.

Devouring of the Marrow (Namira's Maw)
Major Action • Melee Touch 3 FP

The obsidian ribcage unhinges like a bear trap, clamping down onto an adjacent living or freshly slain target to siphon essence directly into the iron soul.

Roll Might (TN 14) or Magic (TN 19). Deals 14 Irreducible Entropic Damage. Alfonso recovers +4 FP and scrubs 2 Surface Rust.

Lightning Discharge
Major Action • 60ft Range 3 FP

Channels raw galvanic arc energy through the crystalline lattice of his spine.

Bonus Actions & Reactions (FROGS 5)

Transmutation of Life
Bonus Action / Free • 1/Turn 0 FP (Free)

Before casting any spell, Alfonso may bypass the FP cost entirely by voluntarily taking Surface Rust / Fracture equal to the spell's FP cost. Allows limitless casting at the cost of the vessel's structural integrity.

Shatter-Guard
Reaction • On Struck 2 FP

Alfonso angles his crystalline forearm to catch an incoming physical blow. Gains +6 DR against the strike. If the weapon is non-magical metal, the attacker must roll Standard Might (+0) or their weapon notches (-1 weapon damage).

Corrosive Parry
Reaction • On Targeted 1 FP

Parries an incoming melee attack with the rapier. Contested roll: Alfonso's Magic (TN 19) vs. Attacker's Might/Agility. On success, the attack misses and acidic bile splashes the attacker's weapon/armor, reducing their DR by 2 for the rest of the encounter.
