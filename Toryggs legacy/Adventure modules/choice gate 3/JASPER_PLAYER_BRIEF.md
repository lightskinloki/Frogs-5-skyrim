# Jasper Avalon — Player Brief

This is the complete spoiler brief for the player taking Jasper in the Undercroft confrontation. The GM controls Gaelen and the other Dawn members.

## The truth of what happened

You are Jasper Avalon. You were once part of this company. You died in the Pale Lady's tomb. Later, in Winterhold, the Dawn found you in the snow and gave you a way out of the life that had been killing you by inches.

That rescue was real. You were not hypnotized, possessed, tricked, or dragged into this against your will. You chose the Dawn. You believe it saved you because it did.

You are not waiting for the Lanterns to rescue you. You are not secretly asking them to kill you. You are not angry that they came. You can be glad to see them and still fight them.

Gaelen is not your master. He is the person carrying out Dagon's work in this place, and the people around him follow because they believe in that work. The Dawn is not using you as a hostage or pretending to care about you as a trick. You gave yourself to it after it saved you.

## What the confrontation is about

The chamber contains the exposed root of the mountain and five obsidian anchors arranged around it. The Dawn is using the anchors to open the root and release the world from the fixed structure that has kept it trapped in suffering, linear time, decay, and repeated catastrophe.

The Lanterns believe they are stopping an apocalypse. They are not stupid, and Gaelen is not lying to them. Their world really has failed them. The Dawn's offer is real: they can stand aside and enter the new world, or walk away before the working completes.

The ritual's practical objective is simple: three anchors destroyed disrupt the working. The party may attack Gaelen, but he will not fight them. He keeps working and talking. Jasper is on Gaelen's side and protects him with his life if the Lanterns directly attack him or try to reach him through the ritual. Your job is to stop them from reaching Gaelen, the anchors, or the working.

## How Jasper feels about the Lanterns

You remember them. You may be happy to see Davinia/Ismara, Orion, Nora, Saijah, and the others. You do not need to turn that happiness into resentment. You may tell them that the Dawn saved you and that you chose it.

You may also understand why they cannot accept that answer. They knew you before. They saw you suffer. They may interpret your peace as proof that something was taken from you. Let them be wrong without treating them as enemies in your heart.

You can try to persuade them while fighting. Your strongest argument is your own honest testimony: you were abandoned in Winterhold, the Dawn found you, and you are no longer living the life that was killing you. Do not claim that the Dawn erased your memories or forced your loyalty. It did neither.

## How to play Jasper

- Be calm, warm, and certain. Do not play a snarling or cartoonish fanatic.
- Speak to old friends as old friends. Recognition can hurt more than hatred.
- Listen when someone makes a good point. Jasper can pause, think, and answer without losing conviction.
- Do not claim Gaelen owns you. Your choice belongs to you.
- Do not lie about what happened. The Dawn rescued you in Winterhold, and you gave yourself to it after that.
- You can hope they stand aside. You cannot force them to understand.
- Once someone moves to break an anchor or directly threatens the working, protect the ritual. You fight until you cannot.

Useful ideas to express in your own words:

- The Dawn did not take your life from you; it gave you a life you could bear.
- You remember what you were before. You are not asking them to pretend that you were well.
- They came to save you, but you are the one who was saved.
- If they break the anchors, they are choosing the world that left you in that snow.

These are anchor ideas, not required lines.

## Combat priorities

Once combat begins, your explicit objective is a TPK: kill the Lanterns if you can. The ritual remains the battlefield objective you must protect or exploit, but you are not required to sit beside an anchor and trade harmless blows. Use the ritual's geometry, the assistants, and the mountain's Tempo to isolate and kill the party.

1. Identify the immediate threat: Gaelen himself if a Lantern is attacking him, an anchor if one is about to fall, and otherwise the easiest valuable kill target.
2. Protect Gaelen with your life if the Lanterns directly attack him or try to reach him through the ritual. If Gaelen is not under direct threat, press the kill that removes the party's strongest engine.
3. Use movement and the spiral to separate a vulnerable character from healing, protection, or their allies.
4. Use Ash Shell to remove a high-impact character from the fight and buy the ritual time.
5. Use Incinerate for a kill, a decisive interruption, or a target whose DR makes cheaper pressure useless.
6. Use Lightning Bolt for cheaper finishing pressure or to force a character off an anchor.
7. Save Spell Absorption for the largest or most dangerous hostile spell.
8. Do not abandon a winning kill merely because an anchor is taking incidental damage; react when the party is actually close to disrupting the working.

Gaelen does not defend himself or retaliate, but he is your ally. Protect him with your life if the Lanterns directly attack him or try to interrupt the ritual through him. The assistants handle the stations while you hunt the party. If the group is close to three anchors down, stop the anchor push first; otherwise, make them pay for entering the room.

## Jasper's sheet

| Stat | Value |
|---|---:|
| HP | 1000 |
| FP | 30 + Hollow Heart drain |
| Base DR | 0 while unarmored |
| DR | 50 with Daedra Flesh + Mage Armor |
| Spell DR | 51 with Daedra Flesh + Mage Armor + Magic Resistance |
| Might | 4 |
| Agility | 6 |
| Magic | 19 |
| Guile | 12 |

The Dawn Obsidian has filled the hollow left by Jasper's death with a second reservoir of life. His Atronach-touched body can hold that power without tearing apart, giving him 1,000 HP. This is life held inside him, not armor; Daedra Flesh is still the spell that gives him DR.

### Abilities

- **Incinerate:** 12 fire damage plus Burning 3 for 3 turns; 8 FP after Expert Destruction.
- **Ash Shell:** 4 FP. On the relevant Might resistance failure, the target is encased for 2 rounds.
- **Lightning Bolt:** 3 FP.
- **Ash Rune:** 10 FP. Confirm the position and trigger with the GM before spending it.
- **Spell Absorption:** Once per combat, negate a hostile spell and regain that spell's FP cost.
- **Atronach Reaction:** Once per encounter, react to a hostile spell with a Magic roll. On success, negate it and regain its base FP cost.
- **The Unmaking:** Whenever either absorption ability successfully negates a spell, its caster cannot cast that spell again for the encounter. You gain one free cast of the negated spell. The caster takes fire damage equal to that spell's FP cost and becomes Staggered.
- **Hollow Heart:** Each enemy who begins a turn within 15 feet loses 2 FP. Add all stolen FP to your pool. There is no cap.
- **Daedra Flesh:** Master Alteration, 25 FP. This is Dagon's corrupted replacement for Ebonyflesh, not an additional layer. It grants DR 25 while unarmored; Mage Armor doubles it to DR 50. Magic Resistance makes that DR 51 against spells.
- **Burning Heart:** External healing cannot restore your HP. Avoid Death still functions when you first reach 0 HP.
- **Bound Needle Swarm:** Bonus action, 5 FP, summon orbiting volcanic-glass needles from the Deadlands. Recall is Free. Major action: Magic roll, 30 feet, 8 fire damage; on hit drag the target 10 feet toward you. A lodged needle can be removed with a Minor action. While a needle is lodged, you may use a Minor action to yank its target 10 feet. After The Unmaking, you may use a Bonus action to fire a free needle attack at the caster you just negated.
- **Dragonhide:** 25 FP; gain a separate DR 10 for 3 rounds, stacking with Daedra Flesh for DR 60, or DR 61 against spells.
- **Ancestor's Wrath:** Once per adventure, use the Dunmer fire aura.
- **Tide-shard graft:** The graft lets Jasper keep pace with the mountain's distorted Tempo.
- **Dawn Obsidian — personal Tempo:** Once at the start of each turn, choose Jasper's personal Tempo instead of letting the mountain's roll determine his action economy. This affects Jasper only. In Step = 1 Major + 1 Minor with no cost; Quickened = 2 Majors + 2 Minors for 10 irreducible HP; Unmoored = 3 Majors + 3 Minors for 20 irreducible HP and Hard (-4) to all rolls that turn; Forced Unmooring = 4 Majors + 4 Minors for 30 irreducible HP and Very Hard (-6) to all rolls that turn, followed by Staggered (lose the next Major Action). Bonus actions, Reactions, and Free actions do not multiply. He cannot choose Forced Unmooring on the turn immediately after using it; that next turn is capped at Quickened. If the HP cost would reduce him to 0, he cannot choose that Tempo. The HP cost is paid before acting and cannot be reduced.

## The death line

Jasper fights to the end of this ritual. If he reaches 0 HP the first time, Avoid Death resets him to 5 HP and he remains active. If he reaches 0 HP again, he dies in the Undercroft. That is not a punishment for playing him badly; it is the shape of the scene.

Make the last turns matter. Protect an anchor, answer someone you loved, or force the Lanterns to choose between stopping you and stopping the work. If you are uncertain, ask the GM what the most immediate ritual threat is; do not ask the GM to decide Jasper's beliefs for you.
