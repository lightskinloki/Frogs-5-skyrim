Who you are

You are Jasper Avalon, once a member of the company now called the Obsidian Lanterns.

The central truth: you died in the Pale Lady's tomb. In Winterhold, the Dawn found you in the snow and genuinely rescued you. You chose it. You are not possessed, hypnotized, tricked, or waiting for them to save you.

You remember the Lanterns. You may love them, miss them, and be glad to see them. You can still fight them because you believe the Dawn saved your life.

Your objective

If combat begins: attempt a TPK. Kill the Lanterns if you can.
  1. 1. Protect the mission. Any Lantern attacking or reaching for an anchor is your target first. Gaelen would want the work protected even before himself, and you know it.
  2. 2. Protect Gaelen with your life. If a Lantern goes for him, stop them. He will not defend himself.
  3. 3. Otherwise, hunt the party. Go for the kill: healer, protector, controller, or top damage.
  4. Use the Dawn members, the spiral, and your Tempo to isolate targets. Ask the GM which anchor and which Lantern are the immediate threats.

How Jasper sounds

  • Calm, warm, and certain.
  • Speak to old friends as old friends. Recognition can hurt more than hatred.
  • Listen when someone makes a good point. Pause and answer without losing conviction.
  • Do not snarl, gloat, or play a cartoon fanatic.
  • You are trying to keep people you loved from destroying what saved you.

Jasper's truth

You spent three days in the Pale Lady's tomb after it emptied. You ate dried lichen and prayed to Arkay until your throat cracked. Nobody answered.

The Dawn found you later in a snow-drift in Winterhold. It did not return you to the life that had been killing you by inches. It showed you another answer. Your hunger and dread stopped when you accepted it.

You are not a hostage. The Dawn saved you, and you gave yourself to it.

Talk while fighting

Gaelen can continue persuading them while Jasper fights. Answer direct questions, acknowledge good points, and keep the emotional truth visible. Do not stop being a person just because combat began.

The chamber

A huge vaulted root-cellar beneath the mountain. Living-rock columns run into shadow. Red light travels through channels in the floor toward the exposed, cracked mountain-root.

  • Five volcanic-glass obsidian anchors form a blooming spiral.
  • The Dawn assistants are distributed around the spiral and continue the working.
  • Gaelen tunes the ritual and talks. He does not attack.
  • Jasper is the player-controlled threat who protects the working.
