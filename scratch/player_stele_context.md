# CONTEXT: SCENE 21C — THE CHAINED STELE (PLAYER-FACING INFORMATION ONLY)

This document contains ONLY what the player (Orion's player) knows and experiences at the table, with all GM-only secret lore, authorial commentary, and behind-the-screen trackers excluded.

---

## 1. Orion's Character State (What the Player Holds on His Sheet)
- **Identity & Class:** Vampire assassin (Dark Brotherhood initiate), working as a double-agent for Valerius Caelus.
- **Expertise:** Tonal Engineer. Learned the *Diagnostic Song* from observing Hjolmar Ice-Shaper. He is the party's expert on acoustics, resonance, harmonic frequencies, and tonal diagnostics. He is actively teaching his companion, Nora, the basics of tonal diagnostics.
- **The Core Dilemma:** He wears the *Ring of the Pale Regent* on his finger. It is an irremovable magical focus and tether placed by Valerius. Valerius can see and hear through it. Orion desperately wants to break this leash and kill Valerius, but conventional physical and magical means cannot remove the ring without destroying his hand.
- **Recent Action:** Orion reached out to Molag Bal directly, asking how to kill Valerius.

---

## 2. The Physical Presentation in the Chamber
- The party is moving through the Lost Vault of the Return (an ancient pre-Dragon War sanctuary).
- Orion and Nora stop at a basalt recess in the wall.
- **The Visual Description:**
  - An ancient carved stone stele sits in a reinforced basalt recess, wrapped in heavy iron chains and carved with warding runes.
  - The chains are unusual: each link is torqued tight between slotted iron pegs driven into the basalt at measured intervals—built with the same tension-peg tuning system as the bridge of a lute.
  - The stone beneath is scored with deep circular wear-marks where something heavy was struck against the links repeatedly over centuries.
- **The Inscription:**
  - The wedge-runes carved into the face of the stone are ancient Atmoran.
  - No one in the chamber reads ancient Atmoran. The text cannot be translated on the spot.
- **Nora's Action:**
  - Nora reaches out and taps one of the taut chains with her fingernail.
  - The chain rings with a clean, sustained pitch that fills the alcove and hangs in the air far longer than normal iron can hold a tone.
  - She pulls her hand back and looks at Orion.

---

## 3. Investigation & Skill Checks (What the Player Learns by Interacting)
If Orion's player investigates the chains, strikes them, or uses the Diagnostic Song:
- **On a Standard Magic Roll (+0):**
  - Orion identifies the chain assembly as a tonal instrument—a mechanical resonance frame engineered to produce a specific acoustic frequency when struck.
- **On a Hard Magic Roll (-4):**
  - Orion analyzes the harmonic frequency: it is an *unweaving tone*, calibrated specifically to shatter sympathetic enchantments (such as Daedric bonding foci: rings, amulets, and magical tethers).
- **Physical Symptom:**
  - When the chains ring, the metal of the Ring of the Pale Regent on Orion's finger vibrates a little bit in sympathy with the sound. Nora notices the vibration.

---

## 4. The Whispered Divine Interruption
As Orion examines or strikes the chains, a voice speaks directly and internally into Orion's head (inaudible to anyone else):
- **Molag Bal's Words:**
  *"Do not break it."*  
  *"The ring is mine. He borrowed it. You will give it back to me when I tell you to, and not before. Obey."*

---

## The Question
Based on this information alone (and what Orion holds on his character sheet), will the player be able to figure out the puzzle: that these chains produce an acoustic unweaving frequency designed to shatter sympathetic foci like the Ring of the Pale Regent, presenting him with a viable method to break his leash?
