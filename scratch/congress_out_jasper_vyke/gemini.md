Here are three distinct, player-legal Daedric Gift kits for Jasper. They all sit on top of his base AP 20 sheet, heavily amplify his Atronach/Oblivion nature, and translate the feeling of Elden Ring’s Vyke (a highly capable, lore-drenched "player" character who reached the chaotic fire first) into FROGS 5 mechanics.

### IDEA 1: THE HOLLOWED ATRONACH (The "Anti-Caster" Kit)
**The Vyke Element:** The relentless, inescapable pressure of Madness that drains your resources just by being near him, forcing you to fight on his terms.
**The Fiction:** The Atronach stone normally creates a void that absorbs magic. Dagon, the Prince of Destruction, inverted that void into a starving, active maw. Jasper doesn’t just absorb magic anymore; he actively *unmakes* the potential for it in others. He is the ultimate mage-killer.
**The Mechanics (Dagon's Gift: Heart of Ash):**
*   **Aura of Unmaking (Passive):** Any enemy starting their turn within 15 ft of Jasper loses 2 FP, as the ambient heat actively burns their magicka/stamina. If they have 0 FP, they take 2 irreducible damage instead.
*   **Dagon's Redact (Major, 8 FP):** Jasper forces a target within 30 ft to make a Hard (-4) Magic save. On a failure, they "forget" how to cast their highest-tier spell for the rest of the encounter. Jasper immediately casts that spell back at them as a Free action (paying its normal FP cost himself).
*   **Spiteful Absorption (Bonus/Reaction, 0 FP):** When Jasper successfully triggers his base Atronach Stone or Atronach perk to negate an incoming spell, the unmaking fire lashes back along the magical tether. The caster takes Fire damage equal to the negated spell's FP cost and is **Staggered** (lose next Major).
*   **The Cost/Tell (Ashen Blood):** Jasper’s chest has a literal, burning hole where his heart should be (a visual nod to the Frenzied Flame burning away the insides). Because his body is constantly unmaking itself, he cannot receive healing from any external source (potions or allies). He can only regain HP by triggering his *Avoid Death* or stealing resources.
*   **At the Table:** Forces the party's casters into a terrifying position. They can't just throw spells at him because of *Spiteful Absorption*, and standing near him drains their FP. They have to rely on the archer/melee to crack his DR 16, while the casters run interference on the anchors.

### IDEA 2: THE TRUTH OF THE DAWN (The "Frenzy Flame" Spreader Kit)
**The Vyke Element:** The chaotic, ground-scorching AoE of the Frenzied Flame that induces Madness (Frenzy), turning allies against each other, and Vyke's terrifying command-grab.
**The Fiction:** Jasper wrote that "the mountain itself wants to burn." Dagon gave him the power to show everyone that truth. His fire isn't just Destruction magic; it's a blinding Daedric revelation that shatters the mind and spreads chaos. He is perfectly articulate and content, but dangerously contagious.
**The Mechanics (Dagon's Gift: The Blinding Slate):**
*   **The Mountain's Hunger (Passive):** Whenever a creature takes Fire damage from Jasper’s spells (including his *Ancestor's Wrath* aura or Incinerate's *Burning* DoT), they must make a Guile save (no modifier). On failure, they suffer the **Frenzy** status (must attack nearest creature, friend or foe) on their next turn.
*   **Dawn's Grasp (Bonus/Reaction, 6 FP):** Triggered when an enemy moves into melee range. Jasper initiates a contest (his Magic vs their Agility, applying the AP level-gap penalty). On success, he grabs their face, dealing 8 Fire damage and healing his own HP by 8.
*   **Ignite the Anchor (Minor, 4 FP):** Jasper touches one of the room's obsidian anchor-shards. Until his next turn, the shard radiates a 15-foot aura of intense heat; anyone inside it has their Fire Resistance treated as one step lower, and Jasper's spell costs are reduced by 2 FP while he stands in the aura.
*   **The Cost/Tell (The Melted Mind):** Jasper speaks constantly, murmuring the "truth of the dawn," his eyes molten orange and weeping ash. Because his mind is completely laid bare to the chaos of the Deadlands, he automatically fails all saves against Illusion spells, and takes double damage from psychic/mind-affecting attacks.
*   **At the Table:** Utter chaos. The party has to spread out to avoid Frenzy cascades (where a frenzied PC hits another, spreading the madness). The melee fighters risk getting command-grabbed if they rush him. The party must destroy the anchors not just for the ritual, but because Jasper is actively using them as buff totems.

### IDEA 3: THE OBSIDIAN MIRROR (The "Player-Killer" Counter-Kit)
**The Vyke Element:** The invader who fights exactly like a ruthless player—dodging, punishing your flasks (healing), exploiting the action economy, and using your own tricks against you.
**The Fiction:** Dagon didn't just give Jasper fire; he gave him the power to perfectly reflect and unmake his enemies' strengths. He is a dark mirror to the PCs, showing them what a fully realized, AP 20 Champion looks like when they fight to win. 
**The Mechanics (Dagon's Gift: The Daedric Engine):**
*   **Deadlands Phase (Passive):** Amplifies his Mage Armor. While his Ebonyflesh or Ironflesh is active, his body constantly flickers with red/black static, existing partially out of phase with Mundus. He is completely immune to the **Bleeding** and **Paralyzed** status effects (you cannot bleed or paralyze a hole in reality).
*   **Echo of the End (Minor, 4 FP):** Jasper perfectly copies the last Minor or Bonus action used by an enemy he can see in the previous round, using it against them instantly (e.g., a specific dash, an item use, a quick buff).
*   **Punish the Weakness (Bonus/Reaction, 0 FP + spell cost):** Whenever an enemy within 30 ft uses an item (like a healing potion) or casts a Restoration spell, Jasper instantly casts a single-handed Destruction spell (like his 7 FP *Incinerate*) at them as a Free action *before* their heal resolves.
*   **The Cost/Tell (Chained to the Ritual):** Jasper’s chest is pierced by a miniature obsidian shard, linking him directly to the room's objective. He literally cannot leave the Undercroft. More importantly, his soul is structural to the ritual: every time the party destroys one of the three required anchors, Jasper takes 15 irreducible damage and is **Staggered**.
*   **At the Table:** A highly tactical, brutal fight. The party is punished for typical "safe" player behavior like pausing to heal. However, they are heavily rewarded for engaging with the room's objective: breaking the 300 HP anchors is the best way to deal massive, guaranteed damage to Jasper, turning his own Daedric tether into his biggest weakness.
