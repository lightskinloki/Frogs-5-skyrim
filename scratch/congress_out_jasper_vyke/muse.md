# Jasper Avalon as Dagon's Vyke - Research + 4 Gifts

Frank will not see this live. Written as pitch kit for you to pick from.

### Internet Research - Vyke / Frenzied Flame takeaways

**Who Vyke is:** The closest Tarnished to Elden Lord before the player - the knight on Elden Ring's box cover. Member of Roundtable Hold, called "Roundtable Knight Vyke" / "Vyke the Dragonspear." Favorite of the dragon Lansseax, mastered Ancient Dragon Cult incantations and invented *Vyke's Dragonbolt* (lightning body/spear buff). `fextra: Vyke` + `ScreenRant cover art`. Armor described: "Iron armor singed and blistered by fingers... No other Tarnished was closer to the throne... But without announcement, Vyke traveled far below the capital, and was scorched by the flame of frenzy. Did he make his choice for his maiden, or did some other force lure him?" - Fingerprint set description.

**The fall:** Did not want to sacrifice his Finger Maiden to burn the Erdtree/Thorns. Lured by Shabriri (possessing Yura's corpse) with the line "your flesh can serve as kindling" - offers the Three Fingers deep below Leyndell as an alternative. Embraces the Three Fingers naked behind the Frenzied Flame Proscription - gets fingerprint burns across whole body/face, yellow enflamed eyes. Expected to become Lord of Frenzied Flame, failed, imprisoned in Lord Contender's Evergaol (Mountaintops). His invader ghost still haunts the Church of Inhibition - where his Maiden's corpse lies - as **Festering Fingerprint Vyke**. `Gamerant Vyke story` + `Fextralife Festering Fingerprint Vyke`.

**How the invasion fight plays - why players remember him:**
- Ambush invader on road to Church of Inhibition, through a Frenzied Flame Village with a madness-eye tower. No torrent, sudden.
- Kit: `Vyke's War Spear` (Great Spear, 65 madness buildup per hit flat) + Frenzied Flame Incantations: *The Flame of Frenzy* (eye-flame burst), *Frenzied Burst* (concentrated eye laser, long range, high poise damage), *Howl of Shabriri* (AoE shriek, builds madness on self + nearby foes, then +25% damage dealt / +30% damage taken / draws aggro for 40s).
- *Frenzyflame Thrust* (spear Skill): leap then plunge + yellow explosion leaving 3-pronged Three Fingers flame fork on ground. **Self-inflicts 20 madness** per use, but also builds huge madness on target. `Eldenpedia Frenzyflame Thrust` + `Howl of Shabriri`.
- Heals 1-2x with Flask. Immune to slash meta, parriable but punishes greedy. Madness proc = 100+15% max HP + 30+10% max FP damage + multi-second stun (victim clutches head). In PvE non-Tarnished are immune - in PvP it's lethal. That self-harm + high reward is core fantasy.

**What the Frenzied Flame wants:** Outer God of chaos vs. Greater Will / Two Fingers. The Three Fingers are its burned vassal. Doctrine from Hyetta: "All that there is came from the One Great. Then came fractures... May chaos take the world." The Flame "incinerate[s] all that divides and distinguishes" and melts torment, despair, sin, curse - the "fire that cleanses the slate." To be touched, you strip naked, you are burned, your eyes yellow. Madness buildup lore = self-gouged eyes as "grapes", Focus is the resist stat.

**Midra (DLC secondary reference):** Sage who studied the Flame, told by wife/temptress Nanaya to "Endure." Hornsent executed him not by death but by piercing him with the barbed **Greatsword of Damnation** that blooms inside - eternal agony to suppress the Flame. Endured for years. When finally pushed by the Tarnished he apologizes - "Forgive me, Nanaya" - pulls the sword *through his own head*, decapitates himself, head becomes a fireball: **Lord of Frenzied Flame Phase 2** with delayed greatsword swings + constant madness + arena nuke at 65% HP. Theme: imposed endurance as a curse, then voluntary self-destruction as release.

**Translation principle for Jasper:** Vyke works because he is *a player-shaped character who got there first* - same Tarnished origin, same systems, just higher level and willing to pay the forbidden price. Players think "I could make exactly that build" - and realize the GM can play it better.

### IDEA 1: THE FINGERPRINTED ATRONACH - The Invader

**Vyke element:** Festering Fingerprint invasion loop - every hit builds madness, Frenzied Burst eye laser, Frenzyflame Thrust leap+explosion, Flask heal, fingerprint burns, self-inflicted madness.

**Fiction:** In the drift above Ivarstead, after "Nobody answers," what answered wasn't bread but Oblivion noticing a borehole. The Atronach stone had already thinned him - half-in Oblivion. Dagon didn't have to reach far. He poured his slate-fire into the hole. The fingerprints are still on Jasper's jaw and spear-hand - not a metaphor. Three seared ovals, blistered yellow at the edge, weeping smoke. Jasper is serene about it: the hunger stopped, the dread stopped, because the part that felt them was cauterized. He now calls Gaelen's shards "kindling for the dawn."

**Kit - *Brand of the Three Dawn Fingers***

1. **Hollow Atronach (Passive, amplification of stone):** Jasper is now a living Atronach sigil stone. His two existing negates upgrade:
   - Stone absorption + Alteration perk each recharge on a *different trigger*: one recharges when he is hit by a non-spell elemental attack (fire/frost/shock from weapon/environ), one recharges when an anchor-shard takes >20 damage in one hit (the anchors are part of his circuit).
   - Any spell he negates he may *hold* as a "cinder" instead of immediately regaining FP. Max 2 cinders. He can later burn a cinder for fuel (see 2/3). This is visible - a yellow fingerprint brightens on his chest per cinder.
   - Permanent cost: He *never regains FP on a short rest* (already true) and now also *never regains FP from his own non-absorption effects* (e.g., potions that restore FP fizzle). Only feed is enemy magicka. He is a hunger engine - perfect Mirror to PC gifts.

2. **Frenzied Burst - *Dagon's Eye* (Major, 5 FP, or burn 1 cinder for 0 FP):** Eyes flood yellow. 60 ft line, 5 ft wide. Every creature in line must make a **Hard Guile roll (-4)** or be **Frenzied until end of its next turn** and take **6 fire** (flat, ignores non-magical DR). On success: 3 fire, no Frenzy. This is his "sniper" answer to your strong archer - exactly Vyke's long-range eye laser that punishes casters/archers trying to play at range while he guards anchors.
   - *Self-cost:* Jasper takes **2 fire irreducible** and his next Magic roll is at **-2** (the flame licks the caster too, as all Frenzied incantations do).

3. **Frenzyflame Thrust - *Thrust of the Unmade Dawn* (Major+Minor, 8 FP, or burn 1 cinder for 4 FP):** Jasper sprints 20 ft (does not provoke), leaps, slams a spear of solidified dawn-fire (conjured, not an item) into the ground. 10 ft radius explosion centered on landing:
   - All enemies in radius: **Very Hard Guile (-6)** or **Frenzy (1 round)** + **8 fire**; on success: 4 fire, Slowed (lose next Minor) instead of Frenzy as they clutch head.
   - Ground leaves a 3-pronged scorch (Three Fingers echo) for 2 rounds: entering/starting turn in it = 2 fire.
   - *Self-cost:* Jasper is Staggered next turn if he had no cinder to burn (lose next Major) - the thrust hollows him. This is the Vyke weapon skill's self-madness translated.

4. **Festering Flask (Bonus, 1/combat, reaction to taking damage):** Pulls a cracked stone flask, drinks. Regain **6 HP, 6 FP**, but immediately suffers **Hard Magic roll (-4)** or gain **Frenzy (1 round)** himself as the flame surges. If he frenzies himself he will attack the *nearest* - often an anchor (which he will not want to break) or the nearest PC - creating tactical chaos. This is Vyke's heal, makes him feel like a player with resource management.

**Visible Tell / Exploit:** Fingerprint burns glow brighter per cinder held - you can *see* his charge. Yellow tears smoke constantly; his voice has Shabriri-like doubling. He is **blind in direct sunlight / when both eyes flare** - attacks from his rear arc are **Easy (+2) to hit** the turn after he uses Burst or Thrust (the flame whites out his sight). Party exploit: **Feed him trash spells to overcharge then starve him.** If they stop casting, he has no FP and his best moves stall. Non-spell damage (bow, sword, anchor-smashing) gives him nothing. A PC who *Calms* or *Fears* him can suppress the eye - Calm vs Frenzy interaction is strong per your status list. Seeing the cinders also tells them when to burst anchors while he's empty.

**How It Plays:** Feels like fighting a higher-level invasion build. Casters learn the brutal lesson: every spell you throw can be eaten and shot back as a Frenzy laser. The archer gets dueled at 60 ft by the eye. Melee who closes gets thrust + ground denial around anchors. He fights SMART: holds Mage Armor (Ebonyflesh DR16), saves a negate for the first big PC nuke, then retaliates with free Burst using that cinder. Because he must defend 5 shards (300 HP DR10) and 3 breaks = party win, his positioning is the game - he Thrusts to whichever anchor is being focused. Yet beatable: party can split damage types, make him waste cinders on low-value spells, and punish his blind window.

### IDEA 2: THE ENDURED LORD - The Midra

**Vyke element:** Midra's barbed suppression / transformation + Great Caravan endurance horror. Vyke's "too weak to become Lord" line inverted - Jasper *is* willing.

**Fiction:** Gaelen did not just rescue Jasper - he *crucified* him. A 2-foot obsidian shard - the sixth shard, not counted among the five anchors - is driven through Jasper's sternum, barbs splayed like a branching tree inside his chest, tip out his back. It is why Jasper doesn't shiver anymore; the fire is pinned inside him. He was told "Endure, until the mountain burns" - Nanaya's word as curse. Jasper thinks this is grace. The shard *is* the Atronach stone made physical - an Oblivion anchor lodged in a man.

**Kit - *The Damned Atronach***

1. **Endure (Passive, two-phase):** Jasper starts the fight **Suppressed**:
   - Suppressed: has **+4 FP regen per round** (the shard leaks), but all fire damage he deals is **-4** and he cannot Frenzy anyone.
   - When he is first reduced to 0 HP, **Avoid Death does not trigger as normal**. Instead he rips the shard *forward through his own chest* as a Bonus action, free: shard tears out through his throat/face, he decapitates his own jaw and the wound becomes a hollow sun. He resets to **8 HP** (not 5), sheds Suppressed, enters **Unleashed** for remainder of fight. Narrative Midra beat - the horror is the heal.
   - Unleashed: Loses the regen, but gains: all fire damage **+4**, gains **DR 4 vs non-fire** (fire-head is not flesh), and his Incinerate now also forces **Hard Guile (-4) or Frenzy** on hit (the flame dissolves distinctions). This is the "he is several levels higher after his second wind" moment.

2. **Greatsword of Damnation / Anchor Leash (Passive):** The shard in his chest is tethered to the five anchors by visible heat-haze threads. While Suppressed, **any damage an anchor takes, Jasper takes 1/3 of it (rounded down, irreducible)** - the anchors are *using* him to endure. While Unleashed, this reverses: **any fire damage Jasper takes heals the nearest anchor for half** (the fire returns to source). Party must notice thread shimmer. This makes focus-fire choice tactical - you can bleed him by hitting anchors early, but after transformation you must not feed him fire.

3. **Unendurable Frenzy - *Maw of Dawn* (Major, 6 FP, channeled):** Opens throat-sun and channels as Vyke/Midra's eye-torrent. Can be used while moving 15 ft. Each target in 30 ft cone: **Very Hard Guile (-6)** or take **6 fire + Frenzy (1 round)**; success = 3 fire. Jasper takes **2 irreducible** per round channeled and cannot use Bonus actions while channeling. This is the Midra arena-nuke translated but smaller - devastating if party clumps on an anchor.

4. **Thrashing Barbs (Bonus, reaction, 3 FP, 1/round):** When a melee attacker hits him, barbs lash out from shard-wound. Attacker makes **Hard Agility (-4)** or takes **4 pierce + Slowed (lose next Minor)** as barbs hook. On success half damage, no Slow. In Unleashed form, range becomes 10 ft (fire-whips). This punishes "just rush him" and protects ritual - like Midra's quick/delayed greatsword mix.

**Visible Tell / Exploit:** The shard is *literal* - you can see it. While Suppressed, Jasper is gaunt, stitched, eyes downcast, voice thin; he begs the party to leave ("You don't have to see the dawn"). Barbs glint. When he channels, the whole undercroft hums like a stone in a forge. **Exploit 1:** Observe leash - damage anchors *before* transformation to soften him without triggering his DR, but stop firing anchors after he unleashes or you'll heal them. **Exploit 2:** The shard can be targeted as an object (DR 6, HP 10, **Hard Agility (-4)** to hit without hitting Jasper). Breaking it while Suppressed stuns him (Staggered, lose next Major) and deprives him of the 8 HP transformation - he just dies at 0. High risk/reward archer shot. **Exploit 3:** After unleash, he is **Frenzy-able but immune to Calm** - party can force him to Frenzy and make him attack Gaelen (who never fights) or an anchor, wasting his damage boost.

**How It Plays:** Horror pacing. Starts as sad, enduring mage who absorbs and out-sustains (feels like a player who prepared). Party thinks they're winning by chipping anchors and watching him share damage. Then second phase is pure Lord of Frenzied Flame: fire head, arena becomes unendurable, he plays aggressive - sprinting with Maw + Incinerate Frenzy. Tempo table feels diegetic - shards warping time are literally warping him. Memorable because the transformation is self-inflicted, willing, content ("Forgive me, Master -- I have endured enough"). Beatable because the fight gives a perverse puzzle: manage shard HP vs Jasper HP vs anchor HP.

### IDEA 3: THE SHABRIRI GOSPEL - The Howl

**Vyke element:** Howl of Shabriri (+25% dealt / +30% taken + taunt), Inescapable Frenzy grab, Flame of Frenzy scatter. Shabriri possession / voice that spreads madness through speech. Frenzied Flame Village tower-eye.

**Fiction:** Dagon didn't give Jasper more magicka. He gave him a *voice*. The Atronach stone made Jasper's soul an empty vessel that could *hold* a voice without burning up. Now when Jasper speaks, two voices speak - his own (soft, Dunmer, grateful) and a lower, laughing Shabriri-like overlay that finishes his sentences with "...and all that divides shall burn." His throat is already black, open like a tiny gate to the Deadlands. Every word builds madness. This is the preacher Vyke - Shabriri's disciple - translated to a Mythic Dawn deacon.

**Kit - *Sermon of the Slate-Clean Dawn***

1. **Howl of the First Dawn (Major, 7 FP):** Jasper tilts head back, howls. 20 ft radius from him. All enemies (including allies if any cultists nearby) must make **Hard Guile (-4)** or gain **Frenzy (2 rounds)** and Jasper gains a Body Buff for 3 rounds: **+4 damage to all his attacks but he takes +4 damage from all sources** (the Howl's risk/reward). Even on success, targets take **3 fire + are marked** (attacks against Jasper are Easy +2 next round - the taunt). This is pure Howl of Shabriri - high risk buff, draws aggro, punishes party for clustering to break an anchor. He will cast this *then* Ebonyflesh, so the +4 taken is partly mitigated by DR16, but still scary.

2. **Inescapable Embrace (Bonus, reaction to an enemy failing a Guile save, 4 FP):** Jasper reaches and *grabs* (Magic vs Agility contest, Jasper at **Hard Magic (-4)** due to AP gap mitigated? Actually contest level-gap helps him). On success: **6 fire + target is Frenzied (1 round) + Jasper transfers one negative status from himself to target** (he cleanses his own Slowed/Staggered by giving it away - "all affliction melted away"). This is Inescapable Frenzy's grab - requires he first make you fail a save, rewards setup. Fiction: fingerprint burn appears on target's forehead.

3. **Slate-Cleaning Field (Passive, Atronach-amplified):** Jasper's Atronach nature now projects. At start of each enemy's turn, that enemy's controller must make a **Guile roll (no mod - Easy if they stood still last turn, Hard -4 if they cast a spell last turn)** or the PC hears the second voice whispering "*may chaos take the world*" and loses its **Minor action** that turn (like a mini-madness suppression). Represents the Frenzied Tower eye / pervasive madness aura. Not Frenzy itself, but action economy erosion that makes anchor-breaking slower while Tempo table warps turns.

4. **Scattering Dawn (Major, 6 FP, can be dual-cast with Incinerate for Major+Minor):** Unleashes wild spray of dawn-fire from eyes as in The Flame of Frenzy / Unendurable Frenzy. 30 ft cone, **Hard Guile (-4)** or **4 fire + Frenzy**; success 2 fire. If dual-cast with Incinerate, both happen but Jasper takes **3 irreducible** and is Slowed next Minor (overchannel). This gives him the "player who knows to move while channeling" feel - he strafes while spraying, denying anchor space.

**Visible Tell / Exploit:** His throat-gate glows before Howl (inhale is loud, 2 seconds). Party can **interrupt Howl with any successful attack during wind-up** - if they hit, Howl fizzles and Jasper is Staggered (classic From punish). While buffed, the **+4 damage taken** is exploitable - he *wants* to be hit but his DR is high, so party should answer with many small hits (to get +4 each time) rather than one big hit absorbed by DR. After Howl fades he is hoarse - next Magic roll **Very Hard (-6)**. Also, **silence counters**: a successful Illusion Calm or even a called shot to throat (Hard Agility -4) can suppress his voice for 1 round - reward for fiction-aware play. Permanent price you can see: his eyes are *gone* - he scooped them as an offering (grapes) - empty sockets weeping yellow flame. He navigates by heat, not sight: **Invisible does not work on him, but he cannot read / see glyphs** - party could use written runes to hide.

**How It Plays:** Bard-like battlefield control, not just turret. He feels like a PvP host who uses Shabriri tech: Howl -> buff -> force you to focus him (taunt) -> Inescapable Embrace the one who failed -> Scatter to cover anchor. Tempo table interacts beautifully - he robs Minors while Tempo already messes with turn order, so players feel "madness" as economy loss. Turning Frenzy on PCs is peak Vyke: PCs attack nearest - often each other or the anchor they're trying to protect - so they must space. Yet beatable: interrupt window is generous if archer holds an arrow, and his buff is double-edged; focused fire during buff kills him fast despite DR.

### IDEA 4: THE DRAGONSPEAR ATRONACH - The Battlemage

**Vyke element:** Prime Vyke *before* Frenzy - the respected dragonspear knight vs the later Frenzied invader. This is the "you could build exactly this" option - Vyke dropped his spear build for flame, showing both sides in one fight.

**Fiction:** Dagon gave Jasper not just fire, but *form*. The Atronach stone's void was shaped into a weapon: a 7-foot war spear of translucent dawn-fire and void-glass, humming with absorbed spells. It is both his staff and his spear - the fingerprint burns run *up the haft* like maker's marks. This is Dagon's answer to "forge a champion": give the mage the reach and discipline of the player who actually practices spear + spell, not just spell.

**Kit - *Spear of the Unmade Stone***

1. **Oblivion War Spear (Passive, weapon):** Jasper wields a conjured Great Spear (reach 10 ft). Attacks with **Magic** (spellcraft guiding thrust) for **8 pierce + 4 fire**, on hit builds **Brand** (track Brand 1-2-3 per target; at 3, next hit forces **Hard Guile (-4) or Frenzy** - madness buildup without dice pools). Against spells: spear can **parry spells** - as Bonus reaction, contest **Magic vs attacker's Magic**, if Jasper wins, spell negated and spear gains +2 fire until his next turn (absorbing into blade). This is the Atronach absorption made physical and visible - he doesn't just negate, he *catches* your fireball on the spear tip.

2. **Dragonbolt Reforged - *Dawnbolt* (Bonus, 5 FP):** Wreaths spear and body in dawn-fire for 3 rounds: **+2 damage to all spear/Incinerate damage**, **+10 ft movement**, and his next spell-absorption success also **heals 4 HP** (life leech from void). Visually - red lightning equivalent but yellow-white flame. He will open with this, like Vyke buffing before engagement. Costs his Bonus, so he chooses between this and parry.

3. **Spell-Parry Riposte (Bonus, reaction, costs cinder from 1):** After successfully parrying a spell with spear, may immediately make a free 10 ft thrust (no action) against the caster if in range, or throw spear-flame (30 ft, 4 fire) if not. This is the "fights smart like a high-level player who punishes whiffs" - teaches PCs that casting at him without repositioning is unsafe.

4. **Master of the Elements - Spear Edition (1/adventure as base, but gift augments):** When he uses his 1/adventure round of 0-FP Destruction, he may *also* make two spear attacks at no FP as part of same round. So the nova is: Dawnbolt already up -> 0-FP dual Incinerate (24 fire) + two spear thrusts (8+4 each) in one round. This is the "oh, he's several levels higher" spike - still flat damage, but volume.

**Visible Tell / Exploit:** Spear is loud, bright, leaves afterimage - you always know where he is (no stealth). When Dawnbolt is up, spear *crackles* and his hands smoke - he takes **2 fire per round** from holding it (Frenzied Flame self-damage trope). **Exploit 1:** Spear parry only works **front 180°** - flank/rear spells bypass it (use Tempo distortion to get behind). **Exploit 2:** The spear is conjured from his FP; if he is reduced to **FP 0**, spear shatters for 2 rounds (he's just a robe mage). Party can starve him by forcing him to parry junk, then rush. **Exploit 3:** Reach is his strength but also his tell - he must be within 10 ft to threaten; the strong archer can kite outside 30 ft throw range if they stay mobile. Permanent price: Left hand fused to spear - cannot perform somatic components for Alteration flesh spells without spear in hand - so if spear shatters, his **Ebonyflesh drops to Ironflesh** (DR16 -> DR12) until re-summoned.

**How It Plays:** Most "player mirror" - not a backline turret but a spellblade who closes on your backline, just like invader Vyke sprinting with spear. He feels beatable because his toolkit is *familiar* - you recognize Dawnbolt as "that's just Dragonbolt but fire," spear parry as "that's Spell Absorption but I can see it," and you think "I could take that gift at next patron choice." He protects anchors by body-blocking with reach and riposting anyone who tries to smash while he threatens. Yet lethal because his action economy is tight - Bonus every turn matters - and his 0-FP nova can delete a PC who let Brand stack to 3.

### Comparison & Recommendation

| Gift | Core Fantasy | Lethality | Complexity | Best if you want... |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 Fingerprinted** | Classic invader - absorb and eye-laser retaliate | High vs casters, moderate vs melee | Medium | Purest Vyke invasion feel; teaches "don't feed the mage" |
| **2 Endured Lord** | Midra horror - two-phase crucifixion | Spike at phase 2, puzzle-based | Medium-High | Most memorable set-piece; horror + choice of shard vs anchor damage |
| **3 Shabriri Gospel** | Preacher who steals actions/voice | Control / Frenzy chaos | High | Most Frenzied Flame *ideology* ("all divides melted"); disrupts Tempo economy |
| **4 Dragonspear** | High-level battlemage mirror | Consistent duelist | Medium | Most "I could build that" for players; shows Dagon's champion as complete build |

All hit your brief: Atronach-amplified (each uses negated spells as fuel), Dagon's "fire that cleanses the slate/mountain wants to burn," willing/content Jasper, fight-to-death defender while Gaelen monologues, and beatable by AP12 via damage-type diversity, positioning, interrupts, and anchor management.

If forced to pick one to default: **1 + 2 combined** is strongest: Use **1's** cinder/Frenzied Burst/Thrust as base kit, but *mount the shard from 2* as the visible cost/two-phase trigger. That gives you Vyke's invasion *and* Midra's transformation - exactly what players remember most (the ambush *and* the grotesque reveal). If you want pure control, take **3**. If you want the "GM can play a player better than players" flex, take **4**.

All numbers are flat, d20 roll-under, using Hard/Very Hard Guile/Magic as saves - no new dice - tune up/down by 2 damage if your table runs hotter/colder. Each gift's permanent price mirrors Alfonso's/Namira scale: hollow hunger, stitched crucifixion, scooped eyes, fused hand - Jasper *can't go back* the way PCs can't unsake their patrons.
