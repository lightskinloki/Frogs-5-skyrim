**Board Record — Scene 21C Chained Stele : Can The Player Solve It On Table Information Alone**

### Verdict: YES, but the Hard (-4) gate as currently written is the single point of failure. With one calibration it is watertight without auto-solving.

#### 1. Information Sufficiency
Yes. The chain of clues is complete even stripped of secret lore:

*   **Priming:** Orion's sheet problem is irremovable ring + tonal engineer + Diagnostic Song. Player is actively looking for a ring-breaking method and is trained to think in frequencies.
*   **Diegetic instrument tell:** Tension-peg bridge of a lute + circular strike wear-marks + sustained unnatural ring = not a restraint, it's an instrument meant to be struck repeatedly to produce a tuned tone. This is legible to any player, explicitly legible to a tonal engineer.
*   **Sympathetic link:** Ring vibrates in sympathy when chains ring. That is the puzzle's load-bearing bridge. It converts "interesting instrument over there" to "this instrument affects MY ring."
*   **Divine confirmation:** `Do not break it. The ring is mine.` names the target object and confirms it *can* be broken here. Player does not need to guess what "it" is — Bal does it for him.

Nora tapping it solves the agency problem — player doesn't have to pixel-hunt the interaction.

#### 2. Roll-Gate Risk — HIGH as written
Current split:

*   +0: "this is a resonance frame that produces a specific frequency"
*   -4: "this is an *unweaving* tone that shatters sympathetic enchantments (rings/amulets/blood-tethers)"

If the -4 is the *only* place the word "unweaving/disenchanting/shatter" appears, a failed roll leaves the player with: "it's an instrument" + "my ring wiggled a bit" + "Bal said don't break the ring."

A sharp player will still infer "striking it breaks the ring" from vibration + Bal, but a cautious player will stall at "my ring vibrates — is that good or bad? Does the tone empower it? Is Bal warning me the stele will damage the ring if I hit it wrong?" Without the word *unweaving*, vibration is ambiguous.

**The puzzle cannot require the -4 to be solvable.** Rolls should confirm/accelerate, not gate the core deduction.

Fail-forward is required.

#### 3. Unreadable Runes — Helps, Do Not Change
Leaving Atmoran unreadable is correct. If runes translated to "RING-BREAKING TONE HERE" the puzzle collapses to reading comprehension and bypasses the character's actual expertise (tonal engineering / Diagnostic Song).

Unreadable runes also signals intentionally: *do not solve this with linguistics, solve it with acoustics.* Good negative guidance. Only risk is player fixation on translating — GM should answer any translate attempt with a clean "no one here reads Ancient Atmoran, you can't get it on the spot" and redirect to the chains/ring.

#### 4. Molag Bal Whisper — Clue, Not Distraction, But Wording Matters
Current whisper does two crucial jobs that rescue a failed -4:

1.  Disambiguates "break it" — without `The ring is mine` player could think "don't break the *stele/chains*."
2.  Establishes affordance — if Bal forbids breaking the ring here, breaking the ring here must be possible.

It also correctly creates the *dilemma* vs. mere puzzle: player understands what the chains do, then must choose *when* to use it.

Confusion risk is low unless player reads Bal as "don't ever break the ring" vs. "don't break it *yet*." The addendum `You will give it back to me when I tell you to, and not before` fixes that — it is explicitly temporal, not absolute.

It does not auto-solve tonal details, so player still owns the deduction.

#### 5. Concrete Recommendation — One Tweak

Move the concept of *unweaving/disenchanting* down to the +0, reserve specificity for the -4.

**Change:**

*   **+0 (Standard):** "Resonance frame built to produce an *unweaving/disenchanting* tone — a frequency designed to shake apart sustained enchantments."
*   **-4 (Hard):** "Calibrated specifically for *sympathetic* enchantments — the class that includes Daedric bonding foci: rings, amulets, blood-tethers. Your ring is exactly that class."

**Why this fixes it:** Even on a failed Hard check, player has: instrument + unweaving purpose + ring vibrating + Bal naming ring. Deduction is closed. Hard success then gives certainty/permission to act ("yes, it won't take your hand with it").

If you will not change the check, the alternate single tweak with same effect:

Have Nora explicitly voice the vibration — not GM narration alone: `Nora: "Orion — your ring. When it rang, it moved."` and require GM to describe vibration as *audible/sympathetic hum* not "a little bit." A player can miss boxed text; he cannot miss his apprentice pointing at his hand. This makes the physical clue table-reliable even if the roll fails.

Do not add a second tweak on top of this (do not also make Bal say "that tone would unmake the ring" *and* move the unweaving tell). One fail-safe is enough — two auto-solves.

**Table handling note:** When handing floor to Orion after Nora's tap, GM should restate the two hard facts together: "The tone is still hanging, and your ring is still humming against it." Pairing them in one sentence does the work the -4 would otherwise have to do.
