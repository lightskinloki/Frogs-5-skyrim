# CONGRESS BRIEF: CAN THE PLAYER FIGURE OUT THE CHAINED STELE PUZZLE?

## 1. THE TASK & OBJECTIVE
We are stress-testing a specific investigative tabletop RPG vignette from **Scene 21C** of the FROGS 5 Skyrim campaign (*The Mortal Limit*).

The scene involves a player character (**Orion**, a vampire tonal engineer) and his companion apprentice (**Nora**) at an ancient Atmoran relic known as **The Chained Stele**.

The GM needs to know:
**Can the player figure out what this puzzle means based strictly on the information presented to him alone?**
(Strip away all GM-only secret lore, authorial intent, and narrative notes—evaluate ONLY the clues, sensory details, character abilities, roll results, and divine whispers the player actually experiences at the table.)

---

## 2. THE PLAYER'S STARTING CONTEXT (What's on the Character Sheet & in Player Memory)
- **Character:** Orion.
- **Background & Abilities:**
  - Vampire assassin (Dark Brotherhood initiate), working as an asset/thrall for Valerius Caelus (an apex Vampire Lord).
  - **Tonal Engineer:** Learned the *Diagnostic Song* from observing Hjolmar Ice-Shaper. He understands acoustics, resonance frequencies, harmonics, and sound-based tonal magic. He is actively teaching Nora tonal basics.
- **The Core Problem:**
  - Orion wears the *Ring of the Pale Regent* on his finger. It is an irremovable magical tether and scrying device placed by Valerius. Valerius hears every spoken word live. Orion desperately wants to break this ring and kill Valerius, but conventional physical and magical methods cannot remove it without destroying his hand.
- **Recent Action:**
  - Orion contacted Molag Bal directly asking how to kill Valerius.

---

## 3. WHAT THE PLAYER ACTUALLY SEES & EXPERIENCES IN THE SCENE

### A. The Physical Chamber
- Setting: The Lost Vault of the Return (subterranean Merethic sanctuary).
- In a reinforced basalt recess stands a stone stele wrapped in heavy iron chains.
- **Key Physical Anomalies:**
  1. The chains are torqued tight between slotted iron pegs driven into the basalt at measured intervals—identical to the **tension-peg bridge of a lute**.
  2. The basalt stone beneath is scored with circular wear-marks where something heavy struck the links over and over across centuries.
  3. The wedge-runes carved into the stone are ancient Atmoran. No one present can read ancient Atmoran; the runes cannot be read on the spot.

### B. Nora's Action
- Nora (Orion's student) reaches out and taps one of the taut chains with her fingernail.
- It rings with a clean, sustained pitch that hangs in the air unnaturally long for iron.
- Nora pulls her hand back and looks at Orion, saying nothing.
- *The GM hands the floor to Orion's player.*

### C. Mechanical Investigation (What Orion gets if he tests the chains or uses Diagnostic Song)
- **Standard Magic roll (+0):** Orion identifies the chain assembly as a tonal instrument—a resonance frame built to produce a specific acoustic frequency when struck.
- **Hard Magic roll (-4):** Orion identifies the frequency as an *unweaving tone*—calibrated specifically to shatter sympathetic enchantments (such as Daedric bonding foci: rings, amulets, and blood-tethers).
- **Physical Symptom:** When the chains ring, the Ring of the Pale Regent on Orion's finger vibrates a little bit in sympathy with the sound. Nora notices the vibration.

### D. The Divine Whisper (Internal only to Orion's mind)
As Orion is examining or striking the chains, Molag Bal speaks directly into his head:
> *"Do not break it."*  
> *"The ring is mine. He borrowed it. You will give it back to me when I tell you to, and not before. Obey."*

---

## 4. WHAT THE PLAYER IS SUPPOSED TO DEDUCE
1. The chains are a weaponized tonal instrument that produces an acoustic frequency capable of unweaving sympathetic enchantments.
2. The frequency can shatter the Ring of the Pale Regent without harming his hand.
3. Molag Bal is commanding him *not* to break it, claiming ownership of the ring and demanding obedience, creating a dilemma: break the leash now, or obey Bal to use it against Valerius.

---

## 5. QUESTIONS FOR THE PANEL
Evaluate the viability of this puzzle from a strict tabletop RPG player experience perspective:

1. **Information Sufficiency:** Is the information provided (lute pegs, striking wear-marks, sustained ringing, sympathetic ring vibration, unweaving tone check, and Molag Bal's explicit mention of the ring) enough for a player to connect the chains to breaking his ring?
2. **The Roll-Gate Risk:** The Hard Magic check (-4) gives the specific words *"unweaving tone that shatters sympathetic enchantments (rings, amulets)."* If the player only makes the Standard check (+0) or fails the Hard check, does the physical setup + the ring vibrating + Molag Bal saying *"Do not break it. The ring is mine"* provide enough breadcrumbs to deduce the purpose, or will the player stall?
3. **The Unreadable Runes:** Does leaving the Atmoran runes untranslated hurt the puzzle, or is it better that the mechanical deduction rests on acoustics and tonal engineering rather than reading an inscription?
4. **Molag Bal's Whisper as Clue vs. Distraction:** Does Molag Bal's whisper help the player realize what the chains do (by directly referencing the ring and forbidding him to break it), or does it confuse the player?
5. **Concrete Recommendations:** What single tweak or calibration would make this deduction watertight at the table without auto-solving it for the player?
