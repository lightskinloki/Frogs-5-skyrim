### II. THE THREE EXPANDED DESIGN GAPS
=====================================================================

#### 1. THE FORK (Lanterns Hesitate / Agree)
- **Mechanism:** The Fork is not an active oath; it is a **failure to act**. Every conversational beat or round without an anchor struck allows the ritual to advance.
- **The Ritual Clock:**
  - *Beat 1 (Entry/Opening):* Sap reaches the base of the anchors.
  - *Beat 2 (Personal Pitches & NPC Sways):* Sap climbs halfway up the obsidian. Zone 3 temporal ripples begin leaking into the wider mountain.
  - *Beat 3 (The Unopposed Dawn):* If 0 anchors are attacked, the 3 keystones lock into resonance. Linear time fractures across the Throat.
- **Player Tension:** The party must actively choose between continuing the dialogue (harvesting intelligence) and stopping the clock. If the party is paralyzed by Gaelen's undeniable reasonability, the NPCs will act based on their own imperatives.

#### 2. THE THIRD WAY (Information Harvesting & Jasper's Truth)
- **Jasper Avalon:** Cannot be rescued. In life, Jasper was left behind in the Pale Lady's crypt when the sword was moved; he prayed to Arkay for three days in the dark until his throat cracked, shivering until his fingers turned black. Gaelen pulled him from the snow-drift above Ivarstead. Jasper gave himself willingly because Dagon gave him warmth, purpose, and content emptiness. He is not mind-controlled; he was rescued from mortal misery.
- **The Prior Knowledge:** The party ALREADY read Jasper's signed journal in the vault (Ch. 30:237–249). The shock is seeing him whole, warm, steady, and truly happy.
- **Information Harvesting:** Gaelen has Magic 21 and zero mortal fear. He answers questions with frictionless sincerity.
  - *Topic A: The Carving / Mehrunes' Razor:* Gaelen reveals that mortal souls can be reshaped at an Anvil using the Instrument, carving away limitation (the proof-of-concept for the False Dragonborn ritual).
  - *Topic B: The Three Pillars:* The Throat is Pillar 3 (Time). Eldergleam (Pillar 1 - Life) was already planted. Sildonia works at the College (Pillar 2 - Magic).
  - *Topic C: The Eldergleam Status:* Confirms the root network is actively carrying the Seed across the province.
- **Mechanical Benefit:** Keeping Gaelen engaged in dialogue reduces his coordination with Jasper (Jasper gains Disadvantage on one tactical reaction per round while Gaelen is conversing).

#### 3. THE COMBAT & NPC TENSION TRANSITION
- **Trigger Points:**
  - *Esbern's Strike:* If the party listens too long, Esbern identifies the Thalmor cadence ("They called it unmaking too") and advances with his blade toward an anchor. The party must physically or verbally restrain Esbern if they want to keep talking.
  - *GEAR's Sway:* Gaelen addresses GEAR's Dwemer engineering with genuine peer respect. GEAR begins rolling toward the array to inspect the harmonic resonance. The party must intervene or lose GEAR to the working.
  - *Ylva's Anchor:* Ylva will NOT strike unless Alfonso gives the command. She watches Alfonso.
  - *The Strike:* Once an anchor is hit, Jasper attacks immediately. Gaelen does NOT attack. He continues speaking, mourning the destruction of the craft.

#### 4. JASPER'S WEAPON (Debate Synthesis)
- **Summoning:** Bonus Action, 5 FP, free recall (Bound Bow precedent).
- **Attack:** Major Action, Magic roll, range 30 ft, 8 fire damage.
- **The Drag:** On hit, pulls target 10 ft toward Jasper. While needle is lodged, Minor Action to yank target 10 ft again. Forced movement (cannot pull through anchors or occupied squares).
- **Escape:** Target may spend a Minor Action to remove the lodged needle.
- **Reaction:** After an Unmaking, throw needle at caster as a Bonus Action.
- **Orbit:** Visual representation of his 15-ft aura drain (no cover, no DR).

### IV. RUNNING THE ENCOUNTER: BEAT-BY-BEAT
=====================================================================

#### BEAT 1: THE INVITATION & GEAR'S RECOGNITION
- Gaelen adjusts obsidian shard #1. Welcomes them without turning.
- Acknowledges their climb: "You have climbed seven thousand steps carrying dead things and stolen years."
- Gaelen crouches to GEAR's eye-line, recognizing a Cognitor-Class platform. GEAR gives telemetry; Gaelen highlights how long it's been since GEAR was treated as a mind rather than a cart-wheel.

#### BEAT 2: JASPER'S LIVING PROOF (Davinia & Jasper)
- Jasper Avalon steps into the light. The party knows from the journal he is here, but seeing him whole, warm, un-shivering, and serene shatters the "mindless thrall" expectation.
- Jasper speaks to Davinia: "Look at my hands, Davinia. I'm not shivering anymore. For the first time since I was a boy in Whiterun... I'm warm. Why are you still shivering?"
- Davinia and the party face the reality: Jasper wasn't stolen; he was rescued when the gods left him to freeze.

#### BEAT 3: THE AEDRIC INDICTMENT & THE SURGICAL CUTS
- Gaelen addresses the party's core wounds:
  - **Saijah:** Kynareth's screaming channel vs. the quiet of the unmaking.
  - **Alfonso:** Projected obsidian frame; Peryite's living decay vs. the Dragon Cult's frozen stasis.
  - **Orion:** Sympathetic tether of the Ring; in unmade time, fixed spatial anchors dissolve.
  - **Davinia:** The weight of the Crown, the sacrifice of memories for a dying Empire.

#### BEAT 4: THE THALMOR ECHO & ESBERN'S CHARGE
- Esbern cuts through the warmth: "I spent thirty years listening to Thalmor inquisitors tell me the world was a mistake. Different robes. Same butcher."
- Esbern draws steel and advances on Anchor #1.
- Party must decide: Restrain Esbern to harvest more intelligence, or let him strike and ignite combat.

#### BEAT 5: COMBAT UNDER ZONE 3 TEMPO
- Jasper engages using his needle array (5 FP summon, 8 fire damage, 10 ft drag).
- Gaelen maintains his tuning and conversation mid-combat, non-retaliatory.
- At 3 anchors down: Gaelen phases into broken time with "Hm. Unfinished, then." Jasper fights to the death.
