## 2. Design principles (PROPOSAL)

1. **Gaelen convinces; players decide.** No roll to convince Gaelen. No roll to decide whether Gaelen is right.
2. **Conversation is the action; ritual is the clock.** Listening helps the party understand and helps Gaelen finish.
3. **Manage your own people, not the boss.** The only social uncertainty worth resolving is: what do wavering NPC allies do, and can PCs steady them?
4. **Different tests in one room, not one test repeated.** Borrowed shape from Blue Palace as described in brief: Tullius-like scrutiny, Whispering Court-like multiple NPC interactions, Thalmor-like moral confrontation, Torygg-like final pure-roleplay question.
5. **Visible state, no hidden persuasion HP.** The GM tracks postures openly so players can play around them.

## 3. Table setup (PROPOSAL, runnable)

Set the room physically before a word is spoken:

**A. Anchors / ritual progress track, visible to all.**
Use 6 boxes or tokens in the open. Do not hide it.
Examples of visible beats, in order: light change, sound / vibration, air pressure / smell, first anchor locks, second anchor locks, completion threshold. Pick concrete sensory markers with the GM beforehand so "progress" is filmed, not announced.

**B. People map.**
Gaelen at ritual center, working. Assistants at anchors, working. Jasper near Gaelen or an anchor, working. Party enters together. Do not split NPC companions off-screen; their wavering only matters if witnessed.

**C. State threshold aloud at start.**
GM states the combat threshold once, plainly, in-world: e.g. touching an anchor, casting at the ritual/Jasper/assistant, drawing and advancing on an assistant, seizing ritual components. This implements your "agreed threshold" so combat is a player choice, not a gotcha.

**D. NPC posture board, open.**
For each companion who can waver, place one marker on:

- `HOLDING` — present, listening, not moved
- `SHAKEN` — visibly affected, quiet / asking questions, still with party
- `MOVED` — has stepped toward Gaelen's offer in word or body, will not help stop ritual unless talked down
- `RESOLVED AGAINST` — has actively rejected Gaelen on own grounds

Start (proposal, adjust to GM's locked character docs later): Esbern `RESOLVED AGAINST`, Mila `HOLDING` (hard to move), Bjorn `HOLDING` with known temptation, Alfonso `HOLDING`, Ylva `HOLDING`, GEAR `HOLDING`, Varon `HOLDING`, Saijah/Orion/Nora/Ismara as PCs or PC-adjacent — do not pre-mark PCs.

No numbers. One marker moves only on a visible fictional beat, never on a single cold roll.

## 4. The scene loop (PROPOSAL — the core procedure)

Run in cycles. One cycle = ~5-8 minutes of table time. Do not script order beyond Cycle 1.

**Cycle structure:**

1. **Gaelen attention:** Gaelen speaks with / responds to ONE person. Either he notices pain, or he answers whoever spoke to him. One exchange only. He does not rapid-fire the room.
2. **Room answers:** That person responds. Anyone may briefly interject in character. GM plays assistants continuing work, no speeches from them unless addressed.
3. **Ritual tick:** Advance visible progress if conversation continued without physical interference. Describe one concrete sensory change + what an assistant does. If PCs did something to slow / study / interfere without crossing threshold, resolve that instead of auto-advance.
4. **Posture update (aloud):** If an NPC was genuinely hit, move marker to `SHAKEN` and show it in behavior, not belief-essay. Example function: falls silent, looks away from party, asks Gaelen a real question, steps closer to hear. No monologue about inner state.
5. **Player beat:** Pause for PC action: talk to Gaelen, talk to an NPC, examine ritual, position, prepare, or cross threshold. Then next cycle.

End after 4-6 cycles OR when party acts decisively. Do not run to exhaustion. The ritual clock forces closure.

**Cycle 1 (fixed starter, proposal):** Gaelen acknowledges party arrival while working, states his task in plain pastoral terms — function only, no written line here — then returns to work. This establishes: he will not stop, will not fight, will talk while working.

## 5. How Gaelen chooses / responds (PROPOSAL)

To keep him improvisable and sincere, use three rules:

**a) Respond, don't target.** If a PC or NPC speaks to him sincerely, he answers that person. He does not ignore a direct address to go flip a weaker target.

**b) If no one speaks, notice pain, not leverage.** He addresses visible distress in order the table can see: grief, imposed identity, exhaustion from judgment, need for recognition. One person per cycle. He does not pile on the same person twice in a row unless they re-engage.

**c) No rebuttal game.** If challenged on doctrine/history/Blades enmity, he answers briefly from his sincere view and returns to work and to the suffering person. He does not try to win. Esbern therefore cannot be "beaten" and Gaelen will not try; that contrast is the point.

What Gaelen never does: threaten, mock, bargain with souls, offer proof he doesn't have, claim ownership of Jasper/assistants, stop ritual to duel, defend himself if struck. If struck, procedure: he endures/continues, assistants/Jasper react, conversation continues if party still talks.

## 6. NPC wavering / defection / resistance without persuasion rolls (PROPOSAL)

Use **trigger + window + intervention**, not contested rolls.

**Trigger:** A marker moves `HOLDING -> SHAKEN` only when the fiction you already established in brief is touched in play:
- GEAR: offered recognition / answer to what he is
- Varon: offered relief from pain / imposed identity
- Ylva: critique of judgment lands, then Alfonso's acceptance visibly undercuts it
- Bjorn: end to grief / future loss named truly
- Mila: threat to those she protects made concrete — this hardens, not softens
- Esbern: does not move toward Gaelen; his function is to name cost and ancient enmity

One touch = `SHAKEN` max. Never `HOLDING -> MOVED` in one beat.

**Window:** A `SHAKEN` NPC does not act yet. They show it and go quiet. This creates a player-visible window of 1 cycle for PCs to engage them. If ignored for a full cycle while Gaelen re-engages them, they may move `SHAKEN -> MOVED`.

`MOVED` means: will not help stop ritual, may verbally defend Gaelen, may physically interpose without attacking PCs unless PCs cross threshold. It does NOT mean mind control, permanent conversion, or fighting the party.

**Intervention:** PCs talk their people down by roleplay grounded in shared history. Rolls only if outcome genuinely uncertain AND the PC attempts something specific that could fail (e.g., reading Varon's pain correctly under pressure, physically reaching GEAR before an anchor locks, getting a panicked Mila to hold position).

If a roll is needed:
- Roll once, to resolve the *attempted influence*, not the central moral decision.
- Success = NPC holds / steps back to `HOLDING` for now, or pauses action. Failure = NPC stays `SHAKEN`/`MOVED`, not "joins enemy." No critical persuasion that ends the scene.
- Prefer no roll when roleplay already answered it: if Alfonso vouches for Ylva from established acceptance, that works without dice. If Bjorn names his own reason to resist from established grief-work, that holds without dice.

**Resistance:** Esbern, Mila, Alfonso function as anchors, not targets. Do not make them waver to force balance. Their resistance gives PCs language to use. Bjorn resists after temptation; play temptation first, resistance second, so his hold feels earned.

GM tracking line per NPC: `Name | HOLDING/SHAKEN/MOVED/RESOLVED | what would steady them (1 phrase from canon) | what would move them (1 phrase)`. Fill the parentheticals later from source docs; do not invent new wounds at the table.

## 7. Ritual pressure (PROPOSAL)

Conversation without cost will drift. Make delay cost visible:

- Each completed Gaelen exchange with no physical interference = 1 tick on the 6-box track.
- PC study / questioning of assistants / careful examination without touching = no tick, but consumes the cycle's player beat (tradeoff).
- Active non-violent interference that stops short of threshold (e.g., shouting to break concentration, placing body between, dispel probing without targeting anchor) = contested or uncertain action; resolve with mechanics, success pauses ticks for 1 cycle, does not reverse ritual.
- Destroying / seizing / dispelling an anchor, seizing components, striking Jasper/assistant = crosses threshold: stop clock procedure, start combat procedure, but Gaelen continues.

At 3 ticks and 5 ticks, change the room tangibly so stalling feels real. At 6 ticks, ritual reaches completion threshold — GM must predefine what that means: does it complete, become irreversible, or open final question? Proposal: it opens the final pure-roleplay question under pressure, not instant world-change, so agency survives to the last beat.

## 8. Multiple tests in one room (PROPOSAL — Blue Palace shape)

Do not run Gaelen as single check. Run four different jobs, explicitly:

1. **Scrutiny (Tullius function):** Esbern / Mila press Gaelen on cost and history. PCs must decide what questions matter. No roll; information + moral weight.
2. **Court (Whispering Court function):** 2-3 companions waver in different directions at once. PCs cannot be everywhere. Triage is the test.
3. **Confrontation (Thalmor function):** Gaelen names a true wound about Skyrim / the party's past failures that cannot be refuted by dice. PCs must answer as people, not rolls.
4. **Final question (Torygg function):** Pure roleplay, no roll allowed. Asked when clock is nearly full or party forces decision. Function: *will you let this finish, stop it by force, or take a third path you name now?* Each PC answers by action/statement. NPC `MOVED`/`SHAKEN` states constrain what help/hindrance PCs have.

## 9. Transition to combat (PROPOSAL)

When threshold crossed:

- Call it plainly: threshold crossed, who did what to which anchor/person.
- Assistants + Jasper defend ritual immediately, by belief. They use non-lethal interposition first if plausible, lethal if party persists. They do not grandstand.
- Gaelen does not join combat, does not defend himself, does not order tactics. He continues ritual + speaking if spoken to. If killed/incapacitated, ritual behavior must be pre-ruled by GM: does it collapse, surge, or hang? Decide before play.
- `SHAKEN`/`MOVED` companions do not auto-attack party. `MOVED` may protect ritual / plead / block. `RESOLVED AGAINST` may fight assistants if PCs do. Do not flip allies to enemies by fiat.
- Allow de-escalation: if PCs stop physical action, assistants return to work, Gaelen continues talking. Combat is reversible until anchors destroyed / deaths occur.

## 10. If the party agrees with Gaelen (PROPOSAL — preserve agency)

Agreement must be playable, not a fail state:

- Let them say so plainly and stand down. Do not punish with a twist ("he was lying!") — violates sincerity canon.
- Immediate procedure: ask each PC what letting it finish means for them; ask dissenting NPCs what they do (Esbern will not assist; Mila will protect people from side-effects; Bjorn will grieve but not stop them). Play that split honestly.
- Offer concrete witness/assist roles: hold perimeter, keep shaken NPCs safe, help assistants stabilize, record what happens. Give agreement content beyond watching.
- If only some agree: hold the room on that split. Do not force PvP. Let holders protect ritual while opponents act, with assistants reacting to physical acts only.
- End with cost and record, not applause: what completed, who saw it, who did not consent. That preserves weight without revoking choice.

## 11. What not to do

- No social HP, no "convince Gaelen" track, no mass persuasion roll to end scene.
- No secret flipping of NPCs. All posture moves aloud and visible.
- No Jasper rescue arc. He argues for staying, sincerely, briefly, then works.
- No Gaelen villain speech, no cryptic threats, no debate points.
- No purple metaphysics for ritual progress; physical description only.
- No final twist that removes the choice.

Pre-write before table:

- Threshold sentence.

- Final question function.
- If-attacked / if-anchor-lost rulings.

At table: loop attention → answer → tick → posture aloud → player beat. Stop at decision or 6 ticks.
