# SCENE 21C — THE CHAINED STELE — GM Design

Runnable vignette for Orion at the Chained Stele. For table use directly after 21B (Troll) and the opening of the basalt slab.

---

### 1. SCENE STAGING & READ-ALOUD

**GM Setup:**
Single basalt recess off the main vault, 3m deep. The air is colder here and dry. Previous rolls/tracking for hunger are not in play — Orion is sated, blood vials steady.

**Principle for this scene:** Valerius hears everything spoken aloud through the Ring of the Pale Regent in real time, as if standing beside Orion. The ring gives **no** warming, glow, pulse, or vibration. Molag Bal hears only inside Orion's head; Valerius cannot hear Bal. Bal hears Orion's thoughts.

Track two channels at all times:
*   **SPOKEN = HEARD BY VALERIUS**
*   **THOUGHT/WHISPERED TO ORION ONLY = HEARD BY BAL, NOT VALERIUS**

Use a physical reminder: place the ring hand in view. When Nora or Orion speak aloud about Valerius / the ring / killing the master, tap the table once for yourself — do not telegraph to players.

**Read-Aloud (positive physical description):**

> The basalt folds back into a shallow alcove. A single slab of pale limestone stands upright in the recess, shoulder-height, its edges squared by adze and hammer. Heavy iron chains cross it corner to corner, pulled taut over iron pegs driven into the stone at top and bottom, each peg turned a quarter-twist past tight. The iron sings faintly when the cold moves. Deep wedge-runes cut the face in tight Atmoran script, the grooves still holding flecks of red ochre and stone dust. At the foot of the stele, a skald's carving: a wolf whelp with blood on its muzzle, standing apart from a larger wolf, each with its own shadow.

**GM Detail to keep in mind (not read-aloud):** This is a Five Hundred Companions warding stone, a skald's counter to Molag Bal's broods. The chains are not to hold the stone — they are the instrument. The pegs are bridge pegs. The chains are tuned strings.

**How Nora Approaches — What She Points Out (Apprentice Observations Only):**

Nora does not go to the runes first. She goes to the chains.

1.  She stops a half-step short, cocks her head. Touches nothing.
2.  She points to the pegs: "Look — they turned them. Like tuning pegs."
3.  She points to the chain tension: the links are torqued, quivering, the middle span clear of the stone by a finger's width, humming in the draft.
4.  Only then she kneels to the wedge-runes and translates literally, finger under each line.

This sequencing hands Orion the *physical* puzzle before the *text* puzzle.

---

### 2. NORA'S DIALOGUE & QUESTIONS — THE APPRENTICE PROMPTING THE TEACHER

**Rules for Nora in this scene:**
*   She translates literally. She does not interpret tonal physics.
*   She observes acoustic anomalies and asks the question Orion can answer.
*   She speaks from her want: a future with Orion without being watched. That want is why she says dangerous things aloud.
*   She cannot lie. If Orion signals her to be quiet, she must learn to stop in real time.

**Nora Beat 1 — Literal Translation (she reads, she does not explain):**

> NORA: (finger tracing, halting) " 'Bal's worm whispers that the blood belongs to the father. It is a thrall's lie. Once the whelp has tasted dragon-meat and held his own hate, the blood answers only to the chest it beats in. The whelp is no longer a child — he is a lord of his own wolf-pack.' "
>
> (she looks up) "Whelp... whelp is a young vampire? And dragon-meat — is that feeding on your own? Surviving alone?"

**Speaker Want:** She wants Orion to confirm her reading is right. She is checking her Atmoran against his experience. She is not lecturing.

**Nora Beat 2 — Physical Anomaly Questions (hands puzzle to Orion):**

> NORA: "Why chain a stone? The pegs — someone wrenched them tight after the chains were set. The chains don't even touch in the middle. They ring. Like—" (she mimes a lute string) "— like bridge strings. Did they make it to sound?"
>
> NORA: "And the runes say the blood answers to its own chest now. Is that... is that you? You've fed yourself, you've held your ground. Are you not under him the same way anymore?"

She stops there. She does NOT say "sympathetic tether" or "counter-resonance." She gives Orion: tension + bridge + ringing = acoustic device, and whelp + own hate + own chest = independent blood.

**Nora Beat 3 — Hope Creating Peril (must be managed by Orion):**

> NORA: (quieter, hopeful, dangerous if spoken aloud) "If this stone says how they broke — " (she glances at Orion's hand, the ring) " — could we break — could we have quiet? Just us, to think how to — to deal with him?"

**GM Note:** If she says "Valerius," "the ring," or "kill him" aloud, Valerius hears it live. Orion must intervene before or during this beat — with a gesture, a cut-off, a redirection into tonal metaphor. This is the surveillance pressure point.

**Example Redirection Orion Can Model (do not feed him this line, let him find it):**
Orion speaking in craft terms: "Nora, show me the pitch of that chain," or "Sing the note you hear" — keeps the conversation in tonal physics that Valerius hears as workshop talk, not treason.

If Orion does not stop her and she speaks plainly, do not punish instantly with combat — log it. Valerius now knows they are considering severance at the stele. His counter-move triggers later in the vault.

### 3. PLAYER AGENCY & DIAGNOSTIC PROMPT — HANDING THE FLOOR TO ORION

Orion is the expert. Nora gave him the physical clues. Now the GM turns entirely to him.

**GM Framing (hand the floor, name his expertise):**

> "Orion — you've seen this before, in a different shape. Hjolmar's Diagnostic Song. The chains are under tension over bridges, the stone is a sounding board. Nora's read says the blood of a whelp who has fed and held hate answers to its own chest. You're the tonal engineer here. What do you do? How do you test what this stele is for?"

Wait. Let the player propose the action. Do not prompt the answer.

**Expected Player Move:** Orion uses the Diagnostic Song — hums/sings a tone, taps the chain, listens for sympathetic return through the ring/metal.

**Mechanics — FROGS 5 d20 Roll Under Stat:**

*   Base Stat: **Tonal Engineering** (or Magic/Intelligence if Tonal Engineering not sheeted — use Orion's highest acoustic/craft TN). Roll must be *under* TN.
*   GM applies Difficulty Modifier to TN, never a fixed DC.

Suggested Modifiers:

*   **Recognizing the device is acoustic (chains as tuned strings over bridge pegs):** +2 to +4 (Easy) if player describes inspecting tension/ringing — Nora already gave this, so grant advantage via +2 if Orion engages.
*   **Diagnosing the sympathetic tether (ring is a tuned thread, striking it sends shock back to Sire):** **-2 (Challenging)** — requires Diagnostic Song + active listening. Success = Orion can articulate: "The ring is a sympathetic string. The stele stores a counter-tone that snaps it."
*   **Executing the counter-tone precisely (striking the chain/ring to produce unweaving frequency):** **-4 (Hard)** — requires tools/voice and positioning. On success, leash snaps and backlash stuns Valerius. On failure by 1-3, incomplete snap — ring cracks but holds, Valerius feels the tug. On failure by 4+, mistuned resonance — pain/feedback to Orion only.

Let the player **explain** the physics back to Nora on a successful diagnosis. That explanation is the discovery payoff.

**Two Core Truths Orion Must Deduce/Speak (do not have Nora say them):**

1.  **Lordling, Not Thrall:** The skald's text proves vampiric subjugation after the first independent feed and sustained will is psychological, not physiological. Orion's blood has rooted. He is already a lord of his own pack. The leash holds because he still obeys as a thrall, not because he must.
2.  **The Acoustic Counter-Tone:** A sympathetic focus (ring/amulet) is a bidirectional acoustic thread. The stele's chains are tuned to the unweaving counter-resonance. Striking that frequency into the metal severs the thread and sends a concussive shockwave back up the line to the Sire, stunning him briefly.

If the player gets 1 but not 2, allow Nora to ask: "Then what are the chains tuned to break?" — returning the question.

**If Player Hesitates:**
Prompt with craft, not solution: "What pitch does that chain want? What does your Song tell you when you hold the ring near it?"

### 4. THE DAEDRIC CROSSFIRE — MOLAG BAL vs. VALERIUS SURVEILLANCE

**A. Molag Bal's Internal Whisper — Cold, Plain, Commanding**

Deliver by folded note or quiet lean to Orion's player only. No purple prose, no marrow vibrations, no metaphysical filler. A voice in the head, dry and close.

Use this exact block — deliver in pauses while Nora is speaking aloud:

> *Do not break it like a thrall running from a master.*
>
> *You think to snap the chain and hide. That is fear. I did not choose you for fear.*
>
> *Keep it. Wear what he gave you. Learn to pull on it instead of cutting it.*
>
> *I will show you the first trap. Not the softened thing mortals use to fill gems. The original. Taking a soul whole and dragging it to Coldharbour. That is how you kill a lord who thinks he owns you — you take what is his and keep it.*
>
> *Break the ring now and you blind him for a moment. Keep it and I will teach you to strangle him with it later.*

**GM Notes on Bal's Offer:**
*   Bal is mocking: breaking the ring = thrall behavior.
*   Bal urges corruption/utilization, not flight.
*   **Original Soul Trap** = primordial rite of defilement, pre-mortal-softening. It sends the soul itself to Coldharbour, not into a gem. This is the long path to killing Valerius permanently. It requires time, defilement, and submission to Bal's instruction — cost is explicit.
*   Bal does not argue with physics. He concedes the counter-tone works. He argues it is the wrong *choice*.

**B. Valerius Surveillance — GM Tracking**

*   Valerius hears every spoken word, every hum of the Diagnostic Song, every question Nora asks aloud.
*   He does NOT hear Bal, does NOT hear Orion's thoughts, does NOT get a ring signal.
*   Keep a private tally: Did they say "Valerius / Caelus / master / Sire" + "break / snap / sever / destroy / free" in the same spoken exchange? If YES, Valerius logs intent.
*   Consequence ladder (do not execute instantly in this vignette — set up):
    *   Level 1 (they spoke in metaphor/craft terms): Valerius hears workshop talk, no flag.
    *   Level 2 (they named him and the ring plainly): Valerius knows the location and intent. Next scene, his projection or thrall arrives, or the ring's scrying tightens.
    *   Level 3 (they snap the leash): Immediate backlash — see below.

**C. The Framed Choice for Orion (player-facing, after diagnosis):**

Present plainly, after Orion has explained the counter-tone to Nora:

> "Orion, you have two workings in front of you, both real.
> 1.  **Strike now.** Lay the ring against the taut chain, sound the counter-tone. The thread snaps, Valerius is blinded and stunned where he is — you get silence and breathing room. He will know you cut him, and what you used.
> 2.  **Hold and learn.** Leave the thread intact. Let Bal teach you the Original Soul Trap and how to corrupt the tether Valerius gave you. Slower. You stay watched. But you keep a line to pull on — and you keep the tool that could take his soul whole.
> What do you do? And what do you tell Nora to do — aloud, where he hears, or otherwise?"

Do not let Nora decide. Orion decides, Orion executes, Orion explains.

**If He Strikes Now — Resolution:**

*   Requires Hard (-4) execution roll as above.
*   On success: Sharp metallic crack, chain goes dead, ring shows a hairline fracture and goes inert. Orion feels the snap like a cut string. Far away, Valerius takes a concussive psychic shock — stunned, disoriented for minutes, scrying black. Immediate silence. No retribution in this room — retribution comes as Valerius recovers and understands.
*   Keep the fiction concrete and physical. No light show.

**If He Holds — Resolution:**
*   Ring stays live. Valerius continues to hear.
*   Bal will teach the first step of the Original Soul Trap in the next beat (requires defilement component, not free).
*   Nora learns, viscerally, that Orion is watched every second — she must now practice speaking in craft or silence. This is her terrifying education.

**GM Closing Move:**
End the vignette on Orion's action, not on exposition. If he snapped, end on the sudden absence of weight on the finger. If he held, end on Nora's face as she realizes she was just heard, and Bal's last plain line: *Good. Now keep it.*
