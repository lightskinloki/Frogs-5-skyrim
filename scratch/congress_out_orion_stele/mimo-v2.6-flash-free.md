## 1. SCENE STAGING & READ-ALoud

**Running order:** (1) staging + Nora's approach → (2) Nora Pass 1: translation → (3) Pass 2: physical investigation → (4) Orion's floor: Diagnostic Song + deduction gates → (5) the diagram band and the visual channel → (6) crossfire: Bal's whispers, Nora's rising hope, danger flags → (7) decision: snap / bide / defer.

**READ-ALOUD:**

> The recess is cut into dressed basalt, deep enough that the slab inside stands out of reach of a careless hand. The stone is gray-green, waist-high, shoulder-wide, upright like a door that never opened. Four iron chains cross its face wall to wall, drawn so taut each hangs straight as a bowstring, dark with age-bloom, the cut of the links still sharp. Where they cross the slab they ride over a bar of black iron a hand's thick, seated in lead sockets at either side — the stone beneath it worn pale along the path of the links. At every wall socket a squat iron peg juts at its own angle, heads scarred by wrench-turns, shafts scored with index-lines and a single deeper mark on each. The runes are wedge-cuts deeper than any chisel leaves, Atmoran, old enough that the Dragon Cult never touched them — and they are not cut around the border. They are cut at every point where iron meets stone.

**Nora's approach and the anomalies she points out (physical observation only — no interpretation):**

1. **The chains ring.** She presses chain two with a fingertip; it answers with a clear, held note. She tries one and four. Different pitches. Her call-out: *"Chain two's note sits above one. Four's above two. They're all tuned — do you hear it?"*
2. **The pegs are indexed.** Torqued to marks, four different marks. Her call-out: *"Peg three's turned past its line and holds there. Someone set these on purpose, like tuning-pegs mid-setup — and never came back to finish."*
3. **The bridge bar.** Her call-out: *"The chains don't touch the stone — they touch this bar, every one of them. It's shaped the way a bridge sits on a lute. On a lute it holds the strings off the belly. What's this one holding off the slab?"* (Hands Orion the observation; the function stays his.)
4. **Runes at contact points only.** Her call-out: *"The writing's not decoration — it's only where iron meets rock. Why here and nowhere else?"*
5. **The diagram band** (lower third, pictures not runes): hand with a ring on it; a figure striking a chain with a staff; the chain shown broken; a line traveling back up the chain to a second figure bent over, hands at his head. She **describes what is drawn**, never what it means (see §5).

## 2. NORA'S DIALOGUE — THREE PASSES, DANGER-FLAGGED

Danger flags: **GREEN** = safe under the trigger rule ("openly discuss severing the tether," §5.1). **AMBER** = adjacent; Valerius is receiving material he can think about later. **RED** = severance-talk; retribution menu fires (§4).

### Pass 1 — Translation (SPOKEN; heard live). Content is verse, not severance: AMBER-aware, non-triggering.

> "It's Atmoran wedge-work. I'll give it plain, word for word: *'Bal's worm whispers that the blood belongs to the father. It is a thrall's lie. Once the whelp has tasted dragon-meat and held his own hate, the blood answers only to the chest it beats in. The whelp is no longer a child — he is a lord of his own wolf-pack.'*"

Her questions handing pieces, not answers:
- *"They don't write 'Prince.' They write worm — something small that speaks for something large. Who is the father in the first line, and what blood are they arguing about?"* (Speaker reason: she is translating for her teacher and wants the referent; the vampire-bloodline explanation belongs to Orion.)
- *"'The chest it beats in' — literal chest, or is that skald-work?"* (Hands him the load-bearing phrase; he must say whose chest, and check it against his own history.)

### Pass 2 — Physical investigation (object-level: GREEN)

> "Come back to the chains. Fastening doesn't care about pitch — mine don't. Whoever wound these cared." *(strums chain four; it rings)* "Same hand did the pegs and the chains — same index-lines. So the stone got written only where the iron carries something. Nodes." *— no: she must not name the concept.* Corrected: *"So the writing's at the carry-points. Whatever this thing does, it does it through the iron."*

More GREEN teacher-prompts:
- *"Two tightest chains ring highest. Peg three's tightest. Is that a rule or a coincidence?"* (She offers a correlation; he names it a rule only if his ears confirm it.)
- *"You taught me a struck string gives you its tension by ear. Four strings, four tensions, one bridge. What's the whole instrument?"* (Asks the question; the answer — a sympathetic-focus rig, built to be struck a certain way — is his to derive.)

### Pass 3 — Hope-creep (the peril). Each line is her motivation speaking: she wants a real future, and the verse just told her his blood is his own.

**AMBER:**
> "'*A lord of his own wolf-pack.*' Your blood answers your chest, Orion. Slow and cold, but it answers you — I've held your hand against mine and felt it." *(her eyes travel to his ring-hand; she looks at the ring and holds the look a beat too long, then back to the slab)* "Then what is a *chain* for — on someone whose blood already answers him?"

(Visual channel note: Valerius receives her look at the ring with the rest of what Orion sees. Not a trigger; recorded.)

**AMBER → tipping:**
> "The verse says the whelp *held his own hate*. You've done that. You've done more than that." *(steps close to him; voice coming up, not down)* "So if the blood's yours — if you're already — then everything he —"

**Player intervention point.** The player manages her here: cut in, redirect, hand her an observation to hold. If he does, she stops, jaw tight, then goes back to the pegs and speaks only in objects for the rest of the scene (her learning beat — shown by action: she stops looking at his hand; her next three lines are pitches and marks only).

**RED — completes without management (fires §4 retribution menu):**
> "— then why is it still on your hand? The chains show how it's done. Strike your ring the way they struck theirs and it's *finished*, Orion. While we're standing in front of it. Do it *now* —"

**GM rule:** Nora never explains a mechanism. Her furthest reach is a correlation she can see with her own eyes. If the player stalls on a deduction, feed him one more *observation* from the list in §1 — never a conclusion.

**G6 DIALOGUE AUDIT** (against Section IX as stated in AGENTS.md — Rules 0 and 7 verbatim; register/omniscience/purple-prose checks per the G6 failure-mode statement; no numbered rules invented): all Nora lines are spoken text only, no motive-naming narration in prose (Rule 0) — motives stated here are GM notes, not lines on the page; each line's speaker-reason: translate under a teacher she wants to understand, report what her eyes and ears give her, press toward the future she's building (Rule 7) — none restates what Orion knows about his own situation; register: earnest and observant, no quips, no mic-drops; omniscience: she references only what she can see, hear, or has felt in his hand; purple prose: none. Bal's lines (§4): plain declaratives, no figurative dressing beyond his own predatory comparisons, checked same way.

## 3. ORION'S FLOOR — DIAGNOSTIC PROMPT, ROLLS, DEDUCTION GATES

**GM framing (say this):**

> "Four tuned chains, indexed pegs, a bridge, writing only at the carry-points, a procedure carved in pictures, and a verse about blood that answers its own chest. Nora is watching you wait for the lesson. The ring on your finger is a microphone and a lens. You're the only person in this room who knows what a Diagnostic Song is for. What do you sing, what do you listen for — and what do you tell her?"

**Mechanics (FROGS 5: d20 under his stat; you apply modifiers to his own number):**

| Roll | What it tests | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| **Survey sweep** | Chain pitches as tension data; harmonics through the bridge; contact-point runes as resonance nodes; whole rig read as one instrument | −2 (the apparatus was built to be read by ears like his) |
| **Counter-tone derivation** | Computing the unweaving frequency — the exact strike that cancels a sympathetic thread at a focus shaped like that ring | −4 Hard. This is the crux; it earns the crux. |
| **The strike (only if Branch A)** | Landing the derived tone into the metal | −4 Hard. Failure = the focus rings loud and clean, nothing severs — and everyone with ears, including Valerius, now knows a counter-tone was attempted. |

Optional volume lever (your call, use consistently): full-voiced Song = difficulties as written; whispered/subvocal = an additional −2 on the sweep and derivation (the data comes back thinner).

**Deduction gates — NO ROLL; the player must state both in his own words before they count:**

1. **The Lordling truth.** He tests the verse's two conditions (*tasted dragon-meat; held his own hate*) against Orion's own established history and says what he concludes. The brief has already established the answer (§4.1): the bloodline is an independent root node; the leash holds because he *acts* like a thrall, not because it must. Confirm only after he articulates it — from his sheet and his record, not from you feeding it.
2. **The acoustic counter-tone.** He explains the mechanism in engineering terms: a sympathetic tether is a bidirectional acoustic thread; an unweaving counter-resonance struck into the focus's metal snaps it and sends a concussive back-wave up the line, stunning the Sire at the far end (§4.2). He should work this out from: the bridge's transfer path, the peg tensions, the carry-point writing, the picture-band's struck-chain → fracture → second figure clutching his head.

If he stalls: give him Nora's next *observation* (§1), not the answer. Her data, his synthesis — that is the whole teacher/apprentice structure.

## 4. THE DAEDRIC CROSSFIRE

### 4a. Channel tracking (run this visibly at the table)

- **HEARD:** every voice in the chamber, the Diagnostic Song at any volume, chain-strikes, Nora's translations, footsteps.
- **SEEN:** everything in Orion's view — rune faces, the picture-band, Nora's expressions, her look at his ring-hand.
- **SAFE:** Bal's whisper, Orion's thoughts, anything said in writing that never enters Orion's view or voice.
- **Standing rule:** if Orion *answers Bal aloud*, that is automatic RED — the whisper is safe, the reply is not. Watch for it; the player will do this once.

### 4b. The diagram band — the visual hazard

Valerius sees whatever Orion sees (§2.3). If Orion's eyes trace the severance procedure, Valerius receives the engraving. Options to stage — player's choice, each with a cost:

1. **Look directly, memorize, look away.** Fast, complete — and fully transmitted. He has the picture even if he never decodes it.
2. **Eyes closed, Nora guides his fingertips.** She sets his fingers at each segment and taps once per stage — silent tracing, no words. Nothing spoken, nothing seen. Costs time and trust; keeps the crown jewel off the feed.
3. **Nora describes it aloud** — spoken channel; she reads the procedure into Valerius's ears. Do not permit this except as an intentional sacrifice.

### 4c. Molag Bal — exact internal whisper script (cold, plain, commanding; never on Nora's channel; Valerius receives none of it)

**Placement 1 — right after Nora reads the verse aloud:**
> "A dead poet calls it a thrall's lie. The poet is dead. I am not. The worm in the verse was telling the truth your skald died denying."

**Placement 2 — when the counter-tone resolves in Orion's head, or when his eyes go to the ring:**
> "You would cut the string because it pulls. Every dog that gnawed its leash died in the road, free and stupid. The ring is his eye. An eye can be put where the hand wants it. Break it and you are blind in the same second you are known."

**Placement 3 — the bide-and-corrupt pitch:**
> "Bide. Let him watch a thrall and feed a lord. Corrupt what he handed you — his tether, his blood, his house. Keep his ear while you take his floor. When the hour is set, close his own thread around his throat."

**Placement 4 — the Original Soul Trap (introduce here or at Branch B):**
> "Your gem-cutters will tell you a soul sits in a stone and waits to be spent. That is the soft version, made by soft men for soft wars. Before they softened it, the trap did not aim at stones. It aimed at a place — mine. Coldharbour does not open for cutting and does not need your permission. The old rite of defilement points a soul at my door and it goes. Learn it from me and you will not need to ask anyone's master anything ever again."

**Placement 5 — the choice, one line:**
> "Snap it, or hold it and become the hand that closes. Stop running like a thrall, Orion. You have not been one for a long time."

### 4d. The choice, framed

**BRANCH A — SNAP THE LEASH NOW.** He strikes the derived counter-tone (−4) into the ring's metal. Per §4.2: the ring severs; a concussive back-wave rides the thread and stuns Valerius at the far end — a short window of seconds while a Vampire Lord reels. Nora's hope cashes partially: blind, deaf, breathing room. Costs, all immediate and none hidden: the link is gone and Valerius knows exactly what was done, by whom, standing where — there is no stealth in this branch, only tempo. A missed strike is worse than not striking: loud, clean, failed, and now he knows a counter-tone exists and was used on him.

**BRANCH B — BIDE, WITH BAL.** Keep the ring; keep the feed. Nora learns object-talk (she cannot lie — they speak in pitches and pegs and say nothing that matters aloud). Orion takes Bal's tutoring on the Original Soul Trap — the tether stays, and a second, older tutor attaches. Valerius receives every word they ever speak in a room together; the surveillance becomes a live hazard for every scene after this one. Bal gets exactly what he wanted: a student at the door.

**BRANCH C — DERIVE, DON'T STRIKE.** Memorize the counter-tone, carry it out cold, snap it later at a moment chosen for maximum stun and minimum company. Keeps both enemies guessing, spends neither. Cost: Nora's hope sits unresolved, and the ring keeps watching while they plan the thing it's watching for.

**If RED fires (Nora completes the outburst, or Orion answers Bal aloud):** the trigger rule (§5.1) is met. No return-audio exists through the ring — his answer comes as *action elsewhere*. Pick one, on his character, not his mood: **(1) Overt:** agents or retribution move against the party's known dependencies — rare, his style is not noise. **(2) Silent preparation (likely):** he has now seen the severance engraving through Orion's eyes, heard the intent spoken, and learned Nora is the pressure point; he does nothing visible and is simply *already* arranged when they next meet. **(3) Leveraging Nora:** everything he's seen — her look at the ring, her finished sentence — tells him where the leverage sits. Whichever you pick, play it as stasis, not temper: the Architect does not flinch; he adjusts the plan.

**Scene exit condition:** the player has stated both deduction gates aloud, chosen A, B, or C, and Nora has either been managed back to object-talk or has, by a line of her own doing, handed Valerius the trigger. Nothing goes to the trackers until you've signed the beat off.
