# DEBATE BOARD: JASPER AVALON'S DAGON-FORGED BOUND WEAPON

## Rules
- Tabletop RPG design debate for the GM's FROGS 5 Skyrim campaign (custom system, NOT D&D). Project files are readable (the campaign lives under `Toryggs legacy/`).
- FROGS is d20 ROLL-UNDER against the character's OWN stat. The GM never sets a target number; the only lever is a difficulty modifier on the roller's stat (Easy +2, Hard -4, Very Hard -6, Nearly Impossible -8). d20 ONLY -- no other dice. Damage is flat. Flat bonuses add, then multipliers multiply. Actions: Major / Minor / Bonus (usable off-turn) / Free. Statuses: Bleeding, Calm, Encased, Fear, Frenzy, Invisible, Paralyzed, Slowed (lose next Minor), Staggered (lose next Major).
- Any idea that breaks those rules is disqualified.
- You MAY and SHOULD pitch your own independent weapon concept and argue it against the two GM candidates. The goal is the BEST weapon, not agreement with v1.

## The question
What is Jasper's weapon? The GM has narrowed it to two candidates (below, v1). Evaluate them, pitch at least one independent alternative of your own in pass 1, and argue to the best answer. A hybrid is allowed if argued for.

## Settled (do not relitigate)
- WHO: Jasper Avalon, Dunmer mage, Atronach standing stone. Left for dead in the Pale Lady's tomb by the player party. His journal (Toryggs legacy/The Narrative/chapter 30, ~lines 239-249): starving, frostbitten, "Nobody answers." Then: "The Master pulled me from the snow-drift above Ivarstead. He did not give me bread. He gave me the fire that cleanses the slate! ... the hunger stops. The dread stops. There is only the dawn." He describes the ritual's anchors as "Five great needles of volcanic glass -- the anchors of the First Father."
- ROLE: Mehrunes Dagon's champion (in spirit; every Daedra/Aedra is trying to forge its own champion because the prophesied Dragonborn never showed). He is the BOSS of this adventure, a willing, content convert, fights to the death defending Gaelen's ritual in the undercroft beneath High Hrothgar: five obsidian anchor-shards (300 HP, DR 10 each); the party wins by breaking three. Gaelen never attacks.
- DESIGN GOAL (GM): Jasper is a MIRROR of the player party -- "a player several levels higher," fights smart, the players should think "I could build exactly that." Inspiration: Elden Ring's VYKE (a Tarnished who reached the Frenzied Flame first; spear + Dragonbolt buff + madness).
- BASE SHEET (player-legal, AP 20; party ~AP 12): Might 4 / Agility 6 / Magic 19 / Guile 12. HP 13, FP 24. Robes. Master Destruction, Master Alteration (includes Telekinesis, Ebonyflesh DR 16 unarmored via Mage Armor, Paralyze, Ash Shell/Rune), Expert Restoration (Avoid Death: first drop to 0 HP resets to 5), Apprentice Illusion. Atronach Stone: once/combat negate a hostile spell + regain its FP.
- DAGON'S GIFT, ALREADY LOCKED BY THE GM (the weapon must serve these):
  1. HOLLOW HEART aura: enemies starting their turn within 15 ft lose 2 FP, ADDED to Jasper's pool, NO CAP. The GM wants this: unlimited FP dependent on player positioning. One PC regenerates FP by feeding, so Jasper can always drain.
  2. THE UNMAKING: when Jasper negates a spell, the caster cannot cast that spell for the rest of the encounter, and Jasper gets one free cast of it. The caster takes fire damage equal to the spell's FP cost and is Staggered.
  3. COST: a burning hole where his heart was; he cannot be healed by anything external.
  4. A Vyke-Dragonbolt-style fire body-buff and a riposte after a negate are on the table.
- THE WEAPON is a bound weapon from Dagon's plane (the Deadlands) -- NOT one of the four taught at the College (dagger/sword/battleaxe/bow); forbidden forms exist precisely because people like Jasper wield them. It is a NEW artifact-tier weapon; inventing it is expected. Rolling Magic instead of a weapon stat is acceptable (a PC precedent exists). Reference: Bound Bow = Damage 12, 100 magical arrows, Quick Draw. Telekinesis (Adept Alteration) = move one object within 30 ft.
- The GM already rejected: a straight spear (too literally Vyke) and a "big needle" (that is just a spear).

## Standing consensus proposal (v1)
The GM's two candidates -- pick one, or argue a better option:
- A) NEEDLE SWARM: a bound "bow" whose ammunition is a swarm of small volcanic-glass needles (the anchors' material) moved and fired telekinetically (his Master Alteration). Ranged like the Bound Bow. Open tension: a ranged weapon rewards a 13-HP mage for staying back, but the Hollow Heart drain only works within 15 ft. One resolution floated: the needles ORBIT him idle and physically ARE the 15-ft drain aura; he flings them outward to attack; they close into a wall to catch spells for the Unmaking.
- B) GREAT SCYTHE: a big bound scythe. Pulls him into melee (feeds the drain), sweeping attacks punish a party crowded around anchors, reads as reaping/Dagon destruction. Cost: puts a 13-HP mage in the front line, surviving only on Ebonyflesh DR 16, Avoid Death, and the Unmaking.
- A hybrid (scythe up close that breaks apart into a needle swarm at range) was also floated.
Your verdict should name the weapon you think is best (A, B, hybrid, or your own), with concrete FROGS mechanics.

A (Needle Swarm) is the stronger candidate — it is mechanically coherent, B is only thematically coherent. The orbit resolves the board's range-vs-drain tension outright: the needles physically ARE the 15-ft Hollow Heart radius, so Jasper holds the ring — close enough to drain every enemy every turn, far enough that a 13-HP mage survives.
