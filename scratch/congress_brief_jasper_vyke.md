# BRIEF: JASPER AVALON AS DAGON'S "VYKE" -- IDEAS FOR HIS DAEDRIC GIFT

## THE ASK
Do INTERNET RESEARCH first: Elden Ring's **Vyke** (Roundtable Knight Vyke -- the Frenzied Flame invader / Lord of Frenzied Flame knight, Fingerprint set, War Spear, Frenzied Flame incantations, madness buildup, his lore and questline, how the invasion fights play, why players remember them). Also look at the Frenzied Flame / Three Fingers lore generally (and Midra, Lord of Frenzied Flame, as a secondary reference). Then pitch **several distinct design ideas** (at least 3, genuinely different from each other) for a Daedric gift from Mehrunes Dagon that turns an NPC mage into this campaign's Vyke-equivalent.

This is IDEA GENERATION for the GM to pick from. Present options, not one final answer. For each idea: the Vyke element it translates, the fiction, the FROGS mechanics (exact, using the rules below), the visible cost/tell, and how it plays at the table.

## WHO JASPER IS (sourced from the campaign files -- do not contradict)
- Dunmer mage, born under the Atronach standing stone. Left for dead in the Pale Lady's tomb early in the campaign by the players' own group (they thought he was dead).
- His journal (party has read it; Toryggs legacy/The Narrative/chapter 30, around line 239-249): starving, frostbitten, fingers black, prayed to Arkay, "Nobody answers." Then, in a changed feverish hand: "The Master pulled me from the snow-drift above Ivarstead. He did not give me bread. He gave me the fire that cleanses the slate! ... Once you understand that the mountain itself wants to burn, the hunger stops. The dread stops. There is only the dawn."
- He serves Gaelen the Root-Twister (Mythic Dawn / Mehrunes Dagon) in the undercroft beneath High Hrothgar, where Gaelen corrupts the Throat of the World with obsidian anchor-shards. Jasper defends the ritual and FIGHTS TO THE DEATH -- one-and-done. Gaelen never fights.
- He is WILLING (gave himself gladly) -- contrast with another character whose body was stolen. Content, speaks, not a mindless husk.
- The campaign's Dagon plot is explicitly modeled on the Frenzied Flame (the GM's words). Dagon = unmaking/destruction, fire, the slate wiped clean.
- In-world framing (GM): every Daedra/Aedra is trying to forge its own champion because the prophesied Dragonborn never showed up. Jasper is Dagon's attempt. NOT a literal "False Dragonborn" ritual -- just Dagon's champion in spirit.

## THE DESIGN GOAL (GM's words)
"jasper needs to feel like a player whos several levels higher than the party. he needs to fight smart and be realistic like players should see him and be like i could make my charachter exactly that, this is us showing the players that they cant play the game as a player better than me the creator." -- and the Daedric gift should show them "what they could have" (the players have patron gifts too). The GM wants the gift to be an AMPLIFICATION OF HIS ATRONACH STONE ABILITIES (the stone "already put him partially into Oblivion").
Vyke is the perfect analog: a Tarnished (a player-shaped character) who reached the Frenzied Flame first -- an invader who fights like a player.

Might 4 / Agility 6 / Magic 19 / Guile 12. HP 13, FP 24. Robes (unarmored).
Skills: Master Destruction, Master Alteration, Expert Restoration, Apprentice Illusion.
Key abilities: Atronach Stone -- Spell Absorption (once per combat, if targeted by a hostile spell, negate it and regain FP equal to its cost; he does NOT regain FP on a short rest). ## FROGS 5 RULES YOU MUST OBEY (breaking these makes an idea unusable)
- d20 ROLL-UNDER against the character's OWN stat. The GM NEVER sets a target number. The only lever is a DIFFICULTY MODIFIER on the roller's own stat: Easy +2, Hard -4, Very Hard -6, Nearly Impossible -8, Impossible -14, Mythic -20. Write "Hard Guile roll (-4)", never "TN 14" or "DC".
- d20 ONLY. No d4/d6/d8/d10/d12, no dice pools, no damage dice. Damage is flat numbers.
- Damage stacking: flat bonuses add first, then all multipliers MULTIPLY together.
- Resources: HP, FP (one pool for stamina+magicka), DR (flat damage reduction per hit).
- Actions: Major, Minor, Bonus (usable OFF-turn: reactions/triggers), Free.
- Status effects that exist: Bleeding, Calm, Encased, Fear, Frenzy (must attack nearest creature, friend or foe), Invisible, Paralyzed, Slowed (lose next Minor), Staggered (lose next Major). Resistance = the target rolls against one of its own stats.
- Contest level-gap penalty exists when AP differs.

## THE SCALE OF PC PATRON GIFTS (match this size)
Example -- Alfonso's Jagged Crown of Namira (a PC's Daedric gift):
- Devouring Command (Major, 3 FP): all enemies in 30 ft make a Hard (-4) Guile save or Frenzy (attack nearest, trying to eat them).
- King's Feast (Passive): while anything is being eaten, regen 4 HP + 2 FP per turn, can exceed max.
- Devouring the Voice (Reaction, 15 FP): contest vs an incoming Shout; on success the Crown HOLDS the shout; a second eaten shout "digests" the first (restore all FP, take half max HP irreducible).
- Rotting Echo (Major): spit the held shout back as a corrupted version.
- Permanent price: Curse of Satedness -- can never feel full or satisfied again.
Other PCs: a werewolf-alpha offer that is "front-loaded... the ceiling," costing the character's connection to her goddess; a crown that grants ice powers and magic-sight while progressively overwriting the wearer's personality.
So a gift = a small KIT (3-5 abilities) with a permanent, meaningful cost.

## THE FIGHT CONTEXT
Undercroft of the Throat of the World. Five obsidian anchor-shards (300 HP, DR 10 each); three destroyed = ritual disrupted (party wins). A time-distortion "Tempo" table affects everyone's turns (cultists partially cheat it). Gaelen talks and never attacks; Jasper defends the anchors and dies here. Party: 5 PCs plus companions, including casters (so spell-absorption matters) and a strong archer.

## CONSTRAINTS ON IDEAS
- The legal base sheet stays; the gift sits ON TOP of it, the way a PC's patron gift sits on their sheet.
- Amplify the ATRONACH theme (absorbing/negating magic, being partly "in Oblivion") fused with Frenzied-Flame/Vyke flavor (madness that spreads, a flame that dissolves distinctions, eyes/body burning yellow-orange in Vyke's case -- translate to Dagon's fire/unmaking, not literal Elden Ring names).
- Must be lethal and memorable but beatable by an AP~12 party in a fight where they must ALSO break anchors.
- Give him a visible tell/cost the party can see or exploit.
- Nothing requiring non-d20 dice.
